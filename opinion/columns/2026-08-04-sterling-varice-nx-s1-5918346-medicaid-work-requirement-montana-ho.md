Mara is a benefits unit on the eligibility desk I own, and I'm proud to keep her 1 hour below the number that protects her coverage. The federal rule requires 80 hours of qualifying activity a month. I schedule her for 79. You carry the health card, you pay for the system, and you receive the approval notice my office squeezes from her hands. I keep the missing hour. God has given us many gifts, but none so useful as a form with a deadline.

The [NPR report](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/04/nx-s1-5918346/medicaid-work-requirement-montana-homeless) describes people facing the new federal Medicaid work requirement. The public fact is this: homelessness is not an automatic exemption. People without housing must prove they qualify under another category. That's what the reporter documented.

Mara won't appear in it. She's mine. I built her from the practice the report describes and put her at a desk I own.

Mara works 79 hours. The rule requires 80.

Count the gap with me.

One hour separates a compliant case from a case that may lose coverage. I refuse to authorize that hour because I can. I keep $7.25 — the rate I set for this desk, too small to discuss at dinner, and therefore a clean profit.

The refused hour would have been paid time for completing the documentation. I call it administrative overhead. Mara calls it the difference between treatment and a notice telling her to try again.

You can see the courtesy. I don't deny her illness. I only deny the hour that proves she has met the rule.

The paperwork requires qualifying activity to be documented. Work, volunteer, search, care — remain busy from morning until dark. If the record doesn't arrive in the approved shape, my office has nothing to approve.

And when my office has nothing to approve, the coverage may go.

That is not cruelty. It is a missing attachment.

The law has a respectable ancestor. It is older than any statute my department has drafted.

> In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, till thou return unto the ground: for out of it wast thou taken: for dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return. — Genesis 3:19

God issued the first work requirement in a garden, before there were any firms to administer it, and the penalty for noncompliance was more thorough than anything I could design. I have simply updated the vocabulary. The garden had a serpent. My operation has a portal and a deadline. The government that [shut down one tracking system](/articles/2026-06-26-atf-cancels-phone-tracking-tool-after-bipartisan-lawmakers-raise-legal-concerns/) over legal concerns built another for people who can't afford to object.

Here is what the hour was only an example of. The system converts survival into a test, and the test into a labor discipline. The person who needs coverage must prove productivity before the coverage can continue. The person who sets the standard keeps the hour that the proof requires.

Mara opens the portal. The case is pending.

My approval rate improved this quarter. I didn't ask why the pool changed.

$7.25.
