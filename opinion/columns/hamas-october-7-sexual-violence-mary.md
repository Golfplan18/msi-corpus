The phrase lists themselves, retrieved from the bodies of dead Hamas invaders, Arabic in one column and Hebrew in the other, with *spread your legs* in the third row. Three hundred ninety-six dead at the Nova Music Festival. Kibbutzim emptied. Military bases overrun. The phrase lists were not interpretive material. The phrase lists were operational orders Hamas issued before the operation began.

These are not allegations. The named survivors testified. Forensic pathologists confirmed the physical evidence. The phrase lists were retrieved from the bodies of the operatives Israeli forces killed and from the operatives Israeli forces captured.
