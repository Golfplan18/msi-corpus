*The insecurity you named was the insecurity you made.*

Donald, you stood at a podium on Thursday night and spoke about election integrity while your administration had already cut the offices that safeguard elections. The cyclospora outbreak is in five states. The screwworm is eating into the cattle supply. The wildfire smoke is in the lungs of people who did not vote for you and people who did. You spoke about protecting the country. The country is sicker, weaker, and less protected than when you began.

---

Donald, on July 16 you stood at a podium and said the elections were insecure. You had already fired the three remaining commissioners of the independent federal commission that assists states with election administration — a move this publication [chronicled yesterday](/articles/2026-07-18-trump-fires-election-commissioners-pushes-new-voting-rules-after-speech-alleging/). You had already proposed cutting the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency's budget by $707 million. You had already cost CISA a third of its workforce through staffing cuts. You had already lost as many as 2,800 FBI agents since January 2025. You had already fired dozens of intelligence staff under the acting director of national intelligence. Your administration had spent months building this very list — from the [economy that was supposed to roar and instead stumbled](/articles/2026-03-09-trump-s-roaring-economy-hits-rough-start-with-jobs-gas-markets/), to the elections it was supposed to protect.

Donald, the country is facing a nationwide outbreak of cyclosporiasis, a parasitic illness causing explosive, watery diarrhea that can last weeks. The outbreak in five states was linked to iceberg lettuce served at Taco Bell. Public health officials told reporters their tracking was slowed by the $11.4 billion you cut from state and local health departments. The US is on course for another record year of measles, threatening the elimination status the country gained in 2000 — a status it took twenty-six years to achieve. You have been in office for eighteen months. The measles does not know your schedule. It knows the funding you cut.

Donald, the new world screwworm, a flesh-eating parasitic fly, threatens the cattle supply. Your federal spending reviews held up funding for containment facilities and a $100 million research initiative. You did not create the fly. You gave it the time it needed.

Donald, the administration has rolled back clean air and water standards, repealed the 2009 endangerment finding that serves as the legal foundation for all federal climate regulations, pulled the US from the UN's main climate treaty, shuttered climate research offices, and terminated climate science projects. Canadian wildfire smoke is spreading over swaths of the US. Research shows the pro-fossil fuel agenda has already pushed up greenhouse gas emissions, increasing the severity of wildfires, hurricanes, and other disasters. The smoke is in the lungs of people in New York City today. It does not check party registration before entering the bronchioles.

You spoke about election security while cutting the offices that secure elections. You spoke about protecting the country while making it sick. You spoke about strength while making it weak. This is the pattern you have established: hollow out the thing, then perform concern for the thing you hollowed.

Donald, when you stood at that podium on Thursday night and told the nation its elections were insecure, you had already fired the commissioners whose work was to make them secure. You had already cut the budget of the agency that protects them. You had already sent home the agents who investigate the threats. The insecurity you named was the insecurity you made. You did not discover a problem. You manufactured one, then blamed the machine you destroyed.

Now let me tell you what else you have made.

Donald, the cyclospora outbreak is in five states. People are having explosive diarrhea for weeks. Public health officials cannot track it because you cut the money that tracks it. They cannot warn people quickly enough because you cut the staff that warns them. Your $11.4 billion cut is in the diarrhea of people who ate lettuce at Taco Bell. They did not know they were eating your policy. They thought they were eating lunch.

Donald, the screwworm is eating into the cattle supply. The flesh-eating fly is not a natural disaster. It is a disaster you funded more slowly. The $100 million research initiative you delayed — that delay is in the flesh of the cattle. The containment facility you held up — that delay is in the screwworm's northward march. You did not create the fly. You gave it the time it needed.

Donald, the measles is coming back. The US is on course to lose its elimination status. The status took twenty-six years to achieve. You have been in office for eighteen months. The measles does not know your schedule. It knows the funding you cut.

Donald, the wildfire smoke is in the lungs of people in New York City today. You rolled back the protections that would have slowed the warming that made the fires worse. You repealed the finding that gave the government the authority to regulate the emissions that heat the planet. You shuttered the offices that study the climate. You fired the people who could have told you what was coming. The smoke is in their lungs. It is also in the lungs of people who voted for you. It does not check party registration before entering the bronchioles.

Donald, the record is in your throat. You can feel it when you clear it in the morning. There is a scratch there that was not there a year ago. It is the cuts you signed. The throat knows what the press secretary will not say.

Your hand has not been washed since the budget line that closed the surveillance office. The hand signs the next executive order. The hand does not forget what it has signed. The ink dries. The residue does not. The same hand that waved to the cameras at the podium now grips the pen that will not stop cutting, and the grip has a texture it did not have before. The texture is the closed offices. The texture is the agents you sent home. You can wash the hand. You cannot wash the texture.

Your chest tightens when you read the morning brief about the outbreak you stopped tracking. The tightness has been there for months. You have not named it. You have called it other things — strength, decisiveness, mandate. The tightness does not believe your vocabulary. It tightens on its own schedule.

There is a sourness in your gut that has nothing to do with what you ate. It is the policy coming back to a place the policy cannot reach with its forms and its press releases. The gut has its own ledger. The gut has not signed any executive order. The gut knows.

Your sleep has thinned. You wake at the hours the cough is worst in the cities where the smoke is thickest. You do not know why. Your body knows. The body is not on the schedule you keep. The body is on the schedule of the people you cut, and their schedule is now yours, and you cannot vote your way out of it.

You cannot sign an executive order to make any of this leave. You cannot stage a photo to make it leave. It will stay with you the way the harms you authorized stay with the people who bear them.

You made yourself the strongman. You said you alone could fix it. Donald, you are not the strongman. You are the man who made the country weaker, sicker, and more vulnerable, then stood at a podium and performed concern for the damage you had done.
