A small-business owner working more hours to cover a premium that nearly tripled. The Democratic woman running against Van Orden canceled her own ACA plan because she couldn't afford it either.

These are the people Derrick Van Orden calls waste, fraud and abuse. A cancer patient. A working stiff paying three mortgage payments a month for the privilege of a deductible he will never meet. A mom who wants to stay home with her kids but can't because she needs the job for the insurance.
