*Three tries. One agenda. Zero science.*

Third nominee. That's the headline they hope you skip.

The emails tell the whole story. Kennedy's own chief of staff sent the sitting CDC director an email demanding "political review" of major policy decisions. Not scientific review. Political review of science. He signed it "Make America Great!" like gutting your kid's disease protection was a fucking campaign rally.

Thousands of staff gone. The scientists who tracked outbreaks and reviewed vaccines — pushed out or scared into silence. While they sort out nominee number three, the day-to-day is run by a former Walmart executive. I shit you not. The agency standing between your family and the next pandemic is being managed by retail logistics.

And the nominee to oversee vaccines against pandemics? Sean Kaufman — a guy who's testified defending people who refused COVID vaccines and questioned the safety of universal childhood shots. He'd be in charge of the goddamn countermeasures.

They didn't protect American families. They took a sledgehammer to the one agency that does and called it reform. Kennedy fired the director, installed political commissars over the scientists, and handed the keys to people whose main qualification is never questioning the boss.
