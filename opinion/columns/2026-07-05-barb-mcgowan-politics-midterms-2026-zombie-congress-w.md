You know who else was sick and tired? Me. At the Walmart register. At the kitchen table doing the math. But I still clocked in. I still voted. I still did my goddamn job.

These elephant-branded cowards ran on "we'll drain the swamp" and "America First" and "we'll deliver." What they delivered is a zombie Congress that can't pass a bipartisan spending bill, can't get to the farm bill, can't move defense policy — and is now trying to jam through a third reconciliation package to bypass Democrats entirely because they can't be bothered to do the basic work. What a load of bullshit.

The White House says this is just normal end-of-term energy. Normal. Right. Normal is finishing the job. Normal is not dangling "future roles" in the administration to keep members in line. That last part isn't leadership. That's a leash.

I don't have a White House offering me a job if I keep my mouth shut. I have a union hall my grandfather built, and a family that needed the bargain these freedom-frying hypocrites broke.

Family values? The family-values party can't even show up to work. Take the flag pin off before you go home, you donor-owned dickweeds. You didn't drain the swamp. You are the goddamn swamp.
