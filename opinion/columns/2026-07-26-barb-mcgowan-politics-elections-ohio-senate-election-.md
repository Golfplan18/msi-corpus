*They didn't fight for you. They bought the right to try again.*

I did everything they told us to do. Showed up. Voted for the guy who said he'd fight for working people. And here's what working Ohio got: three Senate terms of jack shit.

Sherrod Brown chaired the goddamn Banking Committee. He had the gavel, the platform, and the promise. Single-payer healthcare? Failed. Tariffs on Chinese imports that killed our manufacturing? Failed. Ban on Wall Street buying up homes families need? Failed. All three.

And when urban Ohio needed the party to show up and organize — to knock doors, turn out voters, fight for every precinct — they didn't bother. Turnout in Ohio's cities barely cracked 60 percent in 2024. Trump country hit 80. The party took those voters for granted, lost the state, and then acted like it was a surprise.

Now Chuck Schumer's crew is back with a $60 million check. Sixty million dollars. Not to pass single-payer. Not to put tariffs on China. Not to stop Wall Street from hoarding homes. That money is for one thing: buying back votes the party spent years refusing to earn.

Brown is out at barns and breweries now, promising to fight for working people. He had three terms to fight for working people. The promises are in the trash. The check is on the table.

That's not a comeback. That's an invoice they expect you to pay twice.

Ohio working people should tell the Democratic establishment to go to hell. You don't get to piss away a decade of promises, lose the state, and then show up with a bag of cash calling yourself the party of working people. That's the oldest con in American politics, and Ohio has seen it before.
