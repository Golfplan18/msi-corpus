The company raised its adjusted earnings outlook to at least $5.25 a share — the same pattern [Elevance Health](/articles/2026-07-15-elevance-health-raises-2026-earnings-outlook-after-q2-sales-beat-estimates/) and [UnitedHealth](/articles/2026-07-16-unitedhealth-beats-wall-street-estimates-and-raises-full-year-earnings-outlook/) showed on the same earnings cycle, raising guidance while their own medical cost ratios climbed. CEO Joseph Zubretsky told investors that "2026 is a trough year for Medicaid pretax margins" and that Molina is "well positioned for profitable growth in 2027." The full-year guidance includes a loss of $1.50 a share from the new Florida Medicaid contract and $1.00 a share from the MAPD product Molina plans to exit. Analysts expected $2.58 in earnings per share; Molina guided to $2.15.
