*They buried the science with the sludge.*

They poisoned the farmland. On purpose.

The waste management industry lobbied against the rules because dumping toxic sludge on cropland is cheap and profitable. The EPA's new position is that sludge isn't a 'wide threat' because it's supposedly only on about 1% of farmland. Except no government agency has ever tracked where the stuff actually goes. They made up a number to protect a profit.

Here's what the EPA's own science said before they burned it: kids drinking milk from contaminated farms — more than 36 out of every thousand could get cancer. Non-cancer health risk jumps 319 times. Every sludge sample Maine and Michigan tested came back positive for PFAS. Every single one.
