The U.S. Department of Justice found that the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation failed to protect incarcerated women from sexual abuse and harassment at the Central California Women's Facility in Chowchilla and the California Institution for Women in Chino. Federal investigators documented numerous instances of guards regularly sexually abusing women at the two facilities. The investigation began during the Biden administration and was released Thursday.

The DOJ found that the department did not provide a safe way for victims to report abuse without retaliation. Staff assigned to investigate misconduct were often themselves accused of sexual abuse or harassment. In at least one case, a guard was convicted of abuse that occurred over roughly a decade and received a 224-year prison sentence.
