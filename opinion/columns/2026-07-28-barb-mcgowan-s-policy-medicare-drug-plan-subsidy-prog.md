*The subsidy kept grandma alive. They called it a problem.*

They're ending the Medicare drug plan subsidy that keeps prescription costs down for seniors, and the Trump administration's own guy told the Wall Street Journal the quiet part: seniors will probably pay more, but they can always go shop around. Go shop around. Like it's a fucking coupon hunt and not your blood pressure medication.

Here's the move. The subsidy encouraged insurers to cover more of the cost, so premiums stayed lower for old people on fixed incomes. The administration says that same subsidy is what let insurers jack up rates because the government was picking up the tab. So their solution is to kill the subsidy, which means the tab just gets handed to the seventy-four-year-old in the pharmacy line who already can't afford her groceries.
