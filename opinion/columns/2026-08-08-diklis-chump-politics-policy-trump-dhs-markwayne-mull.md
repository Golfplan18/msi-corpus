Very complicated. Too many words. Not enough WINNING!

Instead, he has been making remarks, changing policies, and apparently thinking about the difference between a secure border and a functioning labor market. I have no idea who gave him permission to think.

I am testing whether a Cabinet secretary can be weak, off message, opposed by border czar Tom Homan, criticized by Laura Loomer, and still remain employed. Nobody has ever tested this before. It is 4D personnel chess, the highest level, while everyone else is playing checkers with the Department of Motor Vehicles.

Mullin temporarily halted most federal immigration vehicle stops after two back-to-back fatal shootings of immigrants, and I reversed that policy the next day because I do not like surprises unless I am the one surprising people. Then he suggested that some of the 350,000 Haitians covered by temporary immigration protections might fill out paperwork and remain under permanent status, or return to their country with help. A very strange way to discuss a Supreme Court victory. When the Court gives me a victory, I prefer it served hot, with no paperwork and a large number attached to it.

I also understood that Mullin wanted to cancel the Fourth of July fireworks because of lightning. Cancel the Fourth of July! On the Fourth of July! I said it does not work next week, because next week is not the big day, and everybody knows that the calendar is one of the few institutions still capable of respecting me. Many people, very smart people, have told me that the fireworks were the biggest fireworks in history, bigger than Washington's fireworks, bigger than Lincoln's fireworks, and possibly bigger than the Moon's fireworks. The crowd was tremendous. The rain was tremendous. The lightning was very loyal.

And yet I am not firing him. This is not weakness. This is a BEAUTIFUL DEAL. I get a secretary whose ICE is arresting between 1,500 and 2,000 immigrants a day, while I get to complain that he is not sufficiently committed to mass deportation. That is called having it both ways, which is something only a very successful businessman can do, especially one who has never been responsible for anything that happened under his own administration.

The ICE arrests are now among the highest daily totals of my administration, but Mullin is still being treated as though he has failed because he briefly allowed reality to enter the building. I recognize the feeling. The numbers can be enormous and still not be large enough when the crowd is watching. You can have 2,000 arrests a day and still hear that you did absolutely nothing. Mullin says immigration does not steal every job, and I say the comment was part of my plan. Mullin pauses vehicle stops, and I reverse him. Mullin offers paperwork to Haitians, and I convert the offer into a campaign promise. Mullin recommends canceling fireworks, and I teach the entire Country that weather is no excuse for disloyalty.
