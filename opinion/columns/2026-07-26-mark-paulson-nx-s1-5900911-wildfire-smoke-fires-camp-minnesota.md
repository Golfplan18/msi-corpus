The fossil-fuel industry is poisoning the summer for a generation of kids.

Here in Adams County, the air doesn't smell like it used to. The notebook has twelve years of this, and the line doesn't bend back. The seasons are arriving differently. The county fair and the lake and the tree stand used to be the architecture of growing up, and the architecture is changing. The point is not what the notebook records specifically. The point is what the camps are telling us.

A seventeen-year-old girl who'd spent twenty-six days on a backpacking trip in Alberta came back to find the homecoming celebration cancelled. The camp director said they'd never sunk canoes before. They did it because the canoes were too fragile to move to safety.

The American Camp Association says a camp that used to see three or four days with the heat index above 105 now sees eight or ten.
