*£2 bus cap. War-priced gas meter. Same damn scam.*

I read this article and I'm supposed to feel grateful. Andy Burnham's Labour government just cut VAT on electricity bills and capped bus fares at £2. Wow. What generosity. What courage. My heart is full.

Look — I'm American, and I know how this movie ends. You cut a tax on a bill that's already going up. You cap a bus fare so the ride to your second job costs less. You call it 'breathing space.' You tell working people their patron saint finally arrived.

Then the gas meter keeps spinning. Because the US-Iran ceasefire expired without a permanent deal, and the war machine keeps pumping, and the oil price keeps climbing, and the Bank of England held interest rates at 3.75% with inflation already above the 2% target. Analysts say it gets worse by year-end. Defence Secretary John Healey called Britain 'resilient.' Resilient. That's what you call a patient who's still bleeding.

Same scam, different country. The public face is relief. The hidden reality is the war. The receipt is the gas bill.

The Labour Party is not the Republican Party. I'll give them that. But a discount coupon on a rising bill is still a discount coupon on a rising bill. The working people I know don't need breathing space. They need a fucking invoice that doesn't grow every month.

Burnham, Healey, whoever's running the show — take your £2 bus cap and shove it. The pump doesn't care about your VAT cut. The war doesn't care about your press release. And I don't care about 'shared prosperity' from a podium that won't touch the war that's driving the price.

Eat shit. The relief is the costume. The cost is the body.
