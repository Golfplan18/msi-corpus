Chief W.H. "Skip" Holbrook's Columbia Police Department sent a two-year officer to a domestic-dispute call in a park Saturday evening, and a gunfight came back. The 29-year-old officer was a husband and a father of two young children. He did not come home.

Holbrook stood before reporters Saturday evening and asked the city for prayers. "Our hearts are broken this evening," he said. "I hope that our officers know that they're not alone in this. We will lean on each other in this tragic time. Let's pray for this department."

He is the spokesperson for an institution that controls what the public will be told about the choices that produced Saturday's killing, and on Saturday evening he chose to tell the city almost nothing. He did not release the officer's name. He did not take questions. He did not say what domestic-call protocol dispatched the two-year officer rather than a senior partner.
