BRASILIA — Former Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro was temporarily released from federal prison Wednesday to undergo brain tests at a Brasilia hospital after falling from his bed the previous night, according to a court authorization and statements from his family. The 70-year-old returned to prison after the exams, which the hospital said required no additional treatment.

Police escorted Bolsonaro from the federal police's headquarters in Brasilia to the nearby DF Star hospital, where he arrived around midday for three tests: a tomography, a brain scan, and a brain wave test.

At around 4:30 p.m. local time, Bolsonaro's wife, Michelle, said on Instagram that the exams had been completed and that her husband had returned to prison. DF Star hospital later said in a statement that the results showed "mild soft-tissue thickening in the frontal and right temporal regions" due to the trauma, but that no additional treatment was needed.

## Court authorization and medical findings

Supreme Court Justice Alexandre de Moraes authorized the hospital trip based on a health report conducted by the federal police on Tuesday. Bolsonaro had reported mild head trauma, according to that report; upon examination, he was found to be conscious and lucid, with a superficial cut to his face. De Moraes authorized the three tests at the request of Bolsonaro's lawyers and directed that the transfer be conducted in a "discreet manner," with federal police responsible for Bolsonaro's security and his return to prison.

Bolsonaro fell overnight from Monday to Tuesday while sleeping. His wife and his son Carlos said on social media Tuesday that the far-right former politician needed medical attention and expressed frustration that he had not been sent to the hospital the previous day.

## Medical history and conviction

Bolsonaro had previously left the hospital and returned to prison last Thursday, a week after undergoing double hernia surgery. He has been hospitalized multiple times since being stabbed at a campaign event before the 2018 presidential election.

In September, a panel of Supreme Court justices convicted Bolsonaro and several allies of attempting to overthrow Brazil's democratic system following his 2022 election defeat. The plot included plans to kill President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, Vice President Geraldo Alckmin, and de Moraes, as well as plans to encourage an insurrection in early 2023. Bolsonaro was also convicted of leading an armed criminal organization and attempting the violent abolition of the democratic rule of law. He is serving a 27-year sentence. He has denied any wrongdoing.
