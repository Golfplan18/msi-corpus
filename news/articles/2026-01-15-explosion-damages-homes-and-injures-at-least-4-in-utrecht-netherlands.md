UTRECHT, Netherlands — An explosion on a residential street in the center of Utrecht injured at least four people Thursday, an emergency services official said, as firefighters worked to determine whether additional victims remained trapped under rubble. The cause of the blast was not immediately known.

The explosion sent people running into nearby shopping streets, ignited a fire, and left buildings structurally unstable, blocking rescuers from entering the damaged homes, emergency services spokesman Sjaak Haasnoot said.

"It is very difficult at the moment to say how many victims are under the rubble," Haasnoot said. "The fire department cannot enter the building because it is still unstable."

Haasnoot said the four injured people were taken to a hospital. The extent of their injuries was not known, and it was unclear whether there were any additional casualties.

Firefighters were investigating how they could safely comb through the rubble of the shattered buildings to determine whether anyone was trapped, according to the Associated Press. Residents whose homes were damaged were being accommodated in a nearby hotel.

Police were investigating the explosion, Utrecht Mayor Sharon Dijksma told reporters.

"This explosion has had a huge impact on the heart of our city. Everyone is deeply shocked, especially those in the immediate vicinity," Dijksma said.
