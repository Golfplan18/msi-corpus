Two road crashes hours apart in Pakistan killed at least 24 people and injured 45 others on Saturday, officials said. A truck plunged into a canal in Punjab province amid heavy fog, and a passenger bus overturned on a coastal highway in Balochistan.

The first crash occurred in Sargodha, a city in eastern Punjab province, where a truck carrying passengers and cargo skidded off the road and fell into a canal. Heavy fog was a factor, according to police and rescue officials. Fourteen people were killed and nine others were injured.

In a separate crash hours later, a speeding passenger bus overturned on the Makran coastal highway in southwestern Balochistan province, killing at least 10 people and injuring 36 others. The bus was traveling from Karachi to Jiwani, a town in Balochistan, according to Aslam Bangulzai, a senior police official.

Traffic crashes are common in Pakistan and are often blamed on reckless driving, poor road conditions and weak enforcement of traffic laws.
