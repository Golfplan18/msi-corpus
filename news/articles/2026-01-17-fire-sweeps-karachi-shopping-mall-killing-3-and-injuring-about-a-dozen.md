A fire swept through Gul Plaza, a multistory shopping mall in Karachi, Pakistan's largest southern city, late Saturday, killing at least three people and injuring about a dozen others, police and rescue officials said.

Firefighters and rescue workers arrived at the mall shortly after 10 p.m. local time. Television footage showed crews in protective gear battling the blaze using ladders, water cannons, and hoses to douse floors where flames shot out of windows and balconies. Thick black smoke billowed into the night sky and was visible from several blocks away, according to an Associated Press reporter at the scene.

## Rapid spread fueled by stored goods

Authorities said the fire spread rapidly after erupting in an area of the mall where shopkeepers had stored imported garments, clothing, and plastic household goods — materials that helped fuel the blaze. According to local media, most shop owners were closing their stores or had already left when the fire broke out.

The cause of the fire was not immediately known, police said. An investigation would be launched once the blaze was extinguished, police said.

## Widespread fire-safety gaps

Most structures in Karachi and other parts of Pakistan lack fire prevention and firefighting systems, police said, a condition that officials say often results in casualties and property damage. Karachi is the capital of southern Sindh province, where such incidents are common.

In November 2023, a fire tore through another shopping mall in Karachi, killing 10 people and injuring 22 others.
