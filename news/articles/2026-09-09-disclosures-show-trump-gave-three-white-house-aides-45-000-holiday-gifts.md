## Disclosures show Trump gave three White House aides $45,000 holiday gifts

**Subtype:** fact

- President Donald Trump gave holiday gifts of $45,000 each to three White House aides and $20,000 to a fourth, according to annual financial disclosure forms the administration published last week.
- The $45,000 recipients were executive assistant Natalie Harp, communications adviser Margo Martin, and deputy director of Oval Office operations Chamberlain Harris; director of Oval Office operations Walt Nauta received $20,000.
- White House salary disclosures show Harp, Martin, and Harris each earn $150,000 annually; Nauta is paid $175,000 a year.
- A White House official, who was not authorized to comment publicly and spoke on condition of anonymity, said Trump "has a longstanding practice of giving Christmas gifts to people in his orbit, including at times employees and aides, both in government and in his time in the private sector."

## Three $45,000 gifts equal roughly 30 percent of recipients' $150,000 salaries

The four gifts were documented in the aides' annual financial disclosure forms, which the administration published last week.

Harp serves as Trump's executive assistant, Martin as his communications adviser, Harris as deputy director of Oval Office operations, and Nauta as director of Oval Office operations.

At the disclosed salary levels, the $45,000 gifts represent roughly 30 percent of the three recipients' annual compensation. Nauta, who earns $175,000 annually, received $20,000.

In response to the disclosures, the anonymous White House official said Trump "has a longstanding practice of giving Christmas gifts to people in his orbit, including at times employees and aides, both in government and in his time in the private sector."
