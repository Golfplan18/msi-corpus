## Quarterly filing reveals Google's $94.1 billion SpaceX stake after IPO

**Subtype:** fact

- Google disclosed in a quarterly SEC filing that it holds $94.1 billion in SpaceX shares, a roughly 6% stake in the rocket and AI company.
- $80 billion of the shares are subject to short-term restrictions on sale, with an additional $14.1 billion locked through the third quarter of 2027.
- SpaceX shares closed at $112.13 on July 23, giving the company a market capitalization of $1.52 trillion.
- The two companies have discussed a potential rocket-launch deal for orbital data centers as Google expands AI development, The Wall Street Journal reported.

## Most of $94 billion stake subject to share-sale restrictions through 2027

The tech conglomerate's marketable equity securities include $80 billion in SpaceX shares subject to short-term restrictions on the ability to sell and $14.1 billion of shares subject to long-term restrictions through the third quarter of 2027, the filing said. Those lock-up provisions are common for shareholders that buy stock around the time of an initial public offering.

SpaceX debuted on the public markets in June in what Nasdaq said was its biggest listing in history. Shares surged 19% to $135 on their first day of trading June 12, giving the company a market value of $2.1 trillion. The stock has since edged lower; on July 23, SpaceX shares traded at $112.13.

Google has a long history of investing in SpaceX. In May, The Wall Street Journal reported that the two companies were in discussions about a rocket-launch deal to help develop orbital data centers. Google has been expanding its efforts to put computing infrastructure in space as it ramps up AI development, and SpaceX Chief Executive Elon Musk has expressed interest in the unproven technology.
