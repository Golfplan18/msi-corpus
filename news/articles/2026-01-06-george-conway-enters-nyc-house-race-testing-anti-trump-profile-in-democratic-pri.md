The race to succeed Nadler, 78, who announced last year he would not seek reelection and called for generational change in Congress, has drawn several prominent candidates. Conway's entry adds a figure known nationally for his high-profile opposition to Trump and his former marriage to Kellyanne Conway, one of Trump's most prominent defenders during her tenure as senior presidential adviser.

George Conway, 62, a prominent critic of President Donald Trump and co-founder of the anti-Trump Lincoln Project, announced Tuesday that he is running for the U.S. House seat being vacated by retiring Rep. Jerry Nadler in New York City, entering a crowded Democratic primary in a district considered solidly Democratic.

Conway, who worked for years as a Manhattan attorney before relocating to Bethesda, Maryland, said he moved back to the city a few weeks ago after concluding that the race was a viable way to continue his opposition to Trump from Congress. The district covers Midtown Manhattan and the Upper East and Upper West sides.

"This is no ordinary time. And I will not be an ordinary member of Congress," Conway said in a campaign launch video.

### A crowded primary field

Conway joins several other candidates competing to succeed Nadler, 78, who announced last year he would not seek reelection and called for generational change in Congress. The field includes Micah Lasher, a Nadler protégé and state lawmaker; Cameron Kasky, a school shooting survivor and advocate; and Jack Schlossberg, the grandson of President John F. Kennedy, according to the Associated Press.

Conway said he was spurred to run after a conversation with a friend who expressed frustration with some Democrats' decision to vote to end last year's government shutdown. He said he did not want to challenge his congressman in Maryland, Rep. Jamie Raskin, whom he said he loves. A friend then suggested he look at the Manhattan seat.

"It was like, huh, it's an open seat. This isn't crazy. I should think about this," Conway told the AP in an interview.

### A personal and political history with Trump

Conway is a former Republican who supported Trump's 2016 presidential run. He was married to Kellyanne Conway, a pollster and strategist who served as a senior presidential adviser in the first Trump White House and became one of Trump's most prominent defenders. The Conways announced their divorce in 2023, writing in a statement that their marriage had included "many happy years."

As Trump's first term progressed, George Conway became an increasingly outspoken opponent of the president. Trump responded at one point by calling Conway "a stone cold LOSER & husband from hell."

### Running on opposition

In his campaign announcement, Conway positioned his legal background and his years of anti-Trump advocacy as his primary qualifications for the seat.

"This is a moment where we need people who can fight Trump the way he needs to be battled," Conway said, adding that he does not want to be a career politician.

Conway faces the challenge of converting a national media profile and social media following into votes in a district where he has only recently reestablished residence.
