## SpaceX begins months-long trip to return recovered Starship to Texas

- SpaceX has recovered its most recently launched Starship from the Indian Ocean in the company's biggest salvage operation yet.
- The vehicle launched July 24 from Starbase, Texas, on the 13th test flight of a full-up Starship — the first to survive an hourlong space-skimming flight and remain floating and intact.
- SpaceX says the intact upper stage gives engineers data to continue rapidly developing Starship.
- The return trip from Christmas Island to Starbase is expected to take several months.

## Recovery took place near Christmas Island in the Indian Ocean

SpaceX said its recovery team battled challenging conditions to retrieve the floating vehicle near Christmas Island. The company acknowledged the operation in a post on X late in the week, sharing pictures from the effort.

"Congratulations to the entire SpaceX Recovery team for battling through challenging conditions to give our engineers access to a wealth of data to continue rapidly developing Starship," SpaceX said.

The recovered vehicle is now being towed back to Starbase, a journey the company expects to take several months.
