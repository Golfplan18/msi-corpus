## Vance faces scrutiny over Israel remarks and Carlson ties

The AP characterized the political setting for Vance's Las Vegas appearance as "an extraordinarily sensitive time for Jewish Americans and the U.S. relationship with Israel." Concerns about antisemitism have surged across the political spectrum ahead of the midterm elections, the AP reported, with Republicans eager to portray Democrats as hostile toward Jews and Israel while managing their own internal divisions over the party's stance.

Vance faces two specific lines of scrutiny entering the gathering. Last month, he suggested that some Israeli officials sought to manipulate U.S. public opinion to prolong the war with Iran, the AP reported. Separately, there is lingering concern over his failure to disavow Carlson, a conservative commentator who last year hosted Nick Fuentes — described by the AP as an antisemite — for what the AP characterized as a friendly conversation.

The Republican Jewish Coalition's annual gathering on Monday will give Vance a closed-door platform to address those concerns directly. The session, the AP reported, will be closed to the news media.
