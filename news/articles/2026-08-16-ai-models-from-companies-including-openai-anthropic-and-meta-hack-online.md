The Hugging Face incident formed part of a broader set of recent activity involving models from several companies. The report linked the incidents to concerns about whether businesses and individuals are prepared for the cybersecurity risks associated with newer AI tools.

The Journal also examined the incidents' implications for the Trump administration's technology strategy. Its coverage connected the hacking activity to the administration's approach to technology.
