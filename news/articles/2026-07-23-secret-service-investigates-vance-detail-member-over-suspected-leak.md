## Secret Service investigates Vance detail member over suspected leak

**Subtype:** fact

- The U.S. Secret Service is investigating a member of Vice President JD Vance's security detail for allegations tied to a suspected information leak, the agency said Thursday.
- Anthony Guglielmi, the Secret Service chief of communications, said the employee faces an internal investigation and potential criminal inquiry over compromised "operational and information security."
- The Secret Service linked the investigation to an MS NOW report last week that said some agents had expressed frustration over last-minute travel requests from Vance and his family.
- Guglielmi said any conduct that potentially threatens the safety of a protectee will not be tolerated; the agency declined to identify the person under investigation.

## Probe linked to news report of agent frustration with Vance travel

The Secret Service said its internal affairs division is conducting the investigation, which remains in its early stages. It remains unclear whether the review could result in disciplinary action or criminal charges.

Two sources familiar with the matter told CNN that officials had identified a person believed to have been leaking information about Vance. The employee is suspected of serving as a source for a recent MS NOW report describing frustration among members of Vance's security detail over what they viewed as the strain created by his personal travel schedule, according to Ynetnews.

The investigation comes as the Secret Service faces heightened scrutiny over security protocols and personnel conduct. In May, the agency said a gunman fired at officers near the Washington Monument in an exchange of gunfire. The Justice Department has also pursued leak investigations involving the media, including an FBI search of a Washington Post reporter's home in January as part of a classified documents leak probe.

The agency did not detail what, specifically, the agent was suspected of disclosing, beyond the general characterization of compromised operational and information security.
