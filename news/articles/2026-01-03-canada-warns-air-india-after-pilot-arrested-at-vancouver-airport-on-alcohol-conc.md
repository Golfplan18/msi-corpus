Transport Canada warned Air India in a statement Friday that it could lose its Canadian flight authorizations after a pilot was arrested at Vancouver International Airport on Dec. 23 while preparing for a scheduled flight to Delhi. The Royal Canadian Mounted Police made the arrest following what police described as a "report of concern" involving an airline crew member.

The incident prompted Canada's federal transportation regulator to warn that non-compliance with rules barring flight crew from acting within 12 hours of consuming alcohol could result in suspension or cancellation of Air India's Canadian operating documents under the Aeronautics Act.

Transport Canada said it would engage with Air India and Indian aviation authorities to ensure "appropriate followup actions" were taken. The agency noted that India's Directorate General of Civil Aviation held primary responsibility for regulatory oversight of Air India.

The RCMP said an investigation was ongoing and no further information would be released.

## Flight delayed nearly seven hours

The pilot had been preparing for Air India's scheduled daily flight from Vancouver to Delhi, an airport spokeswoman said. The flight was delayed for several hours but later departed safely.

Flight tracking website FlightRadar24 shows the Dec. 23 Air India service from Vancouver to Delhi via Vienna was scheduled to depart at 3 p.m. but did not leave until 10:02 p.m.

## Regulatory consequences

Canadian aviation regulations prohibit pilots and other crew members from acting within 12 hours of drinking alcohol or while under its influence, Transport Canada said. Airlines cannot assign crew who are unfit for duty.

"Non-compliance with these regulations may result in the suspension or cancellation of a Canadian Aviation Document under the Aeronautics Act," the agency said. Airlines could also face judicial and other penalties, Transport Canada said.

Air India did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
