President Donald Trump said Wednesday that he expects acting Attorney General Todd Blanche to become his permanent pick to lead the Justice Department, expressing confidence in the former criminal defense lawyer who has held the acting role. Trump made the statement during an interview on Pod Force One, when the host asked whether Blanche would become the permanent attorney general. "I think he will," Trump replied.

Blanche has occupied the acting attorney general post as Trump's administration continues to staff the Justice Department. Trump's comment Wednesday is the clearest indication to date that he intends to nominate Blanche for a full term, pending the formal appointment and Senate confirmation process.
