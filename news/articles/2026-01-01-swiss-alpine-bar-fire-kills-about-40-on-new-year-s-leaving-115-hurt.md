CRANS-MONTANA, Switzerland — A fire tore through a packed basement bar in this Swiss Alpine resort town during New Year's celebrations early Thursday, killing about 40 people and injuring 115 more — the majority of them seriously — in one of the worst disasters in modern Swiss history, police said.

The blaze at Le Constellation nightclub broke out less than two hours after midnight. Switzerland declared five days of national mourning.

Frédéric Gisler, commander of the Valais cantonal police, said at a news conference that authorities were working to identify victims and notify families, describing the community as "devastated." He warned that identification would "take several days."

"This night should have been a moment of celebration and togetherness, but it became a nightmare," said Mathias Reynard, head of the Valais regional government.

## Cause under investigation

Cantonal prosecutor Beatrice Pilloud said investigators had not yet been able to enter the burned building and that it was too early to determine the cause of the fire.

"At no point was the possibility of an attack of any kind raised," Pilloud said.

She added that the number of people inside the bar at the time of the fire was "at the moment totally unknown," and that the venue's maximum capacity would be part of the investigation. No one had been arrested, she said. "An investigation has been opened — not against anyone, but to clarify the circumstances of this dramatic fire."

## Witnesses describe the scene

Axel Clavier, a 16-year-old from Paris who survived the fire, told The Associated Press there was "total chaos" inside the bar. One of his friends died, and "two or three" were missing.

Clavier said he did not notice when the fire started but saw waitstaff carrying champagne bottles with sparklers. He said he initially hid behind a table, then ran up stairs and used a table to break through a plexiglass window, escaping through the opening when its frame gave way.

He lost his jacket, shoes, phone and bank card while fleeing. "I'm still alive and they're just things," he said. "I'm still in shock."

Two women told French broadcaster BFMTV they witnessed a waiter lift a waitress onto his shoulders while she held a lit candle in a champagne bottle. Flames spread rapidly to the wooden ceiling, they said, and the structure collapsed.

A witness described a stampede as partygoers tried to escape through a narrow staircase and a small door from the basement nightclub. Another told the broadcaster that people were breaking windows to flee — some with serious injuries — while panicked parents arrived by car to search for their children. He said he watched from across the street as roughly 20 people struggled through smoke and flames.

## Italian nationals among the victims

Italy's ambassador to Switzerland, Gian Lorenzo Cornado, told state broadcaster RAI that 13 of the injured were Italian and that six Italians were missing. Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani planned to travel to the site Friday given the significant number of Italian nationals involved.

Three injured patients were being transferred from the Sion hospital in Switzerland to the Niguarda hospital in Milan, Italy's civil protection agency said.

Among the missing was Giovanni Tamburi, an Italian whose mother, Carla Masielli, appealed to media to circulate his photograph.

"We've called all the hospitals but no one tells us anything. We don't know if he's among the dead. We don't know if he's among the missing," she said. "They won't tell us anything."

## Hospitals at capacity

The intensive care unit and operating rooms at the regional hospital reached capacity quickly, Reynard said. Authorities asked local residents to exercise caution in the coming days to avoid accidents that would further strain medical resources already overwhelmed by the disaster.

## A resort at the heart of the Swiss Alps

Crans-Montana sits in the Valais region at roughly 3,000 meters — about 9,850 feet — among the snow-covered peaks and pine forests of the Swiss Alps. The resort is a regular stop on the ski World Cup circuit and is scheduled to host leading male and female downhill competitors in the final events before the Milan-Cortina Winter Olympics in February. The Crans-sur-Sierre golf club hosts the European Masters each August.

## Swiss president responds

Swiss President Guy Parmelin, in remarks on his first day in office, said emergency personnel had been "confronted with indescribable scenes of violence and anguish."

"This Thursday must be a moment of prayer, unity and dignity," Parmelin said. "Switzerland is a strong country, not because it is protected from dramas, but because it knows how to face them with courage and a spirit of mutual aid."

## Historical context

The Crans-Montana fire occurred 25 years after a New Year's Eve blaze in the Dutch fishing town of Volendam killed 14 people and injured more than 200 during a celebration at a café.
