## Nearly $2,000 iPhone Duo will be available on 23 October

The phone takes inspiration from the wide screens of iPads. When opened, it has Apple’s largest phone display and is the company’s thinnest phone. The Guardian described the Duo as the most significant change to the iPhone since its 2007 introduction.

Competitors have released foldable smartphones since the late 2010s, but the Duo is Apple’s first. The Guardian said the rollout is the first major test for Ternus, who took over from Tim Cook last week.
