The show planted Ralph Lauren at the intersection of fashion and Olympic pageantry weeks before the Winter Games, while K-pop star Mark Lee's arrival drew hundreds of fans outside the palazzo and underscored how broadly the brand is now casting its cultural reach.

MILAN — Ralph Lauren presented its winter runway collection at the brand's Milan palazzo on Friday, drawing a celebrity-packed front row that included Emmy winner Colman Domingo, actor Liam Hemsworth, and "Stranger Things" star Noah Schnapp. The fashion house also announced it will outfit Team USA for the opening and closing ceremonies of the 2026 Winter Olympics, scheduled for Feb. 6–22 in the Milan–Cortina region.

David Lauren, the brand's brand and innovation leader, said he will return to Milan to oversee the Team USA fitting for the Olympic ceremony looks. The Americana-themed collection that took the runway featured layered outerwear, Texan suiting, hand-knit ski sweaters, Navajo prints, and mountaineer boots.

Domingo, who arrived fresh off the Golden Globes red carpet wearing Boucheron stud earrings paired with a three-piece suit and an overcoat flung over his shoulders, said the range captured the full sweep of American style.

"We were watching sort of Texas tuxedos and cowboy hats, cowboy boots, we're looking at Navajo prints, at beautiful jewelry," Domingo said. "Things that look vintage and things that are looking forward. We're looking at mountaineer boots — which I thought was very bold to put in the conversation as sort of effortlessly luxurious."

The actor, who won an Emmy for "Euphoria," said the collection felt "very modern" and added: "I think that is the most beautiful expression of Ralph Lauren. You feel the aspirational American values in your clothing."

K-pop star Mark Lee's arrival produced a different scene outside the palazzo entirely. Hundreds of screaming fans rushed his vehicle as he stepped out; he gave them a heart sign before making his way inside. Lee, who was born in Canada, said K-pop's global scale continues to catch him off guard.

"It surprises me even to this day. I was born in North America, and even then K-pop wasn't this big," Lee said from the front row. "I am honored to be able to be a part of this phenomenon. I feel like it's kind of my responsibility to share the good influences that I have in K-pop, for the world."

Noah Schnapp, 21, a University of Pennsylvania senior, said he had just wrapped "Stranger Things'" final season and is now reading scripts looking beyond the sci-fi world. He said he planned to return to Italy within weeks to watch Olympic ice hockey games alongside his Canadian-born parents.

"They made sure I was educated in the hockey culture," Schnapp said.

Singer Nick Jonas and actor Tom Hiddleston flanked David Lauren during the runway presentation. Hemsworth said he took a break from the ski slopes to attend.
