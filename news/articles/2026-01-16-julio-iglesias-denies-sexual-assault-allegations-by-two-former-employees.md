Grammy-winning singer Julio Iglesias denied on Friday that he had sexually assaulted two former employees, calling the allegations "absolutely untrue" in a statement posted on Instagram. The 82-year-old Spanish singer responded after a joint investigation by Spanish outlet elDiario.es and Univision Noticias, published Jan. 13, alleged he sexually and physically assaulted two women who worked at his residences in the Dominican Republic and the Bahamas between January and October 2021.

A Spanish high court received formal allegations against Iglesias on Jan. 5, and Spanish prosecutors said they were studying the matter following publication of the investigation. The women are represented by Women's Link Worldwide, which said they have accused Iglesias of sexual harassment and human trafficking for forced labor and servitude.

"With deep sorrow, I respond to the accusations made by two people who previously worked at my home. I deny having abused, coerced or disrespected any woman. These accusations are absolutely false and cause me great sadness," Iglesias said in the post.

Women's Link Worldwide said the two women had presented their complaint to the Madrid-based high court and were accusing Iglesias of "crimes against sexual freedom and indemnity such as sexual harassment" and of "human trafficking for the purpose of forced labor and servitude." The court has jurisdiction to try alleged crimes committed by Spanish citizens while abroad, according to its press office.

The joint investigation by elDiario.es and Univision Noticias was the product of three years of reporting. Spanish prosecutors announced they were studying the allegations the day after the investigation was published.

Iglesias, 82, is among the world's most successful musical artists, having sold more than 300 million records in more than a dozen languages. He built his career in Spain before achieving wide popularity in the United States and internationally during the 1970s and 1980s. He won a Grammy for Best Latin Pop Performance in 1988 for his album "Un Hombre Solo" and received a Lifetime Achievement Award at the Grammys in 2019. He is the father of pop singer Enrique Iglesias.

In his Instagram post, Iglesias added: "I had never experienced such malice, but I still have the strength for people to know the full truth and to defend my dignity against such a serious affront."
