## Californios becomes world's first Mexican restaurant with three Michelin stars

**Subtype:** fact

- Californios, chef Val Cantú's San Francisco tasting menu restaurant, has become the first Mexican restaurant in the world to earn three Michelin stars.
- Cantú received the third star at a June ceremony in San Diego, capping a trajectory that began with the restaurant opening with its first star in 2015 and adding a second in 2018.
- Cantú told the Associated Press that the eight-year wait had been "really rewarding" and that his 40-person team now carries "a little bit more confidence in their step."

## Cantú says team carries more confidence after historic third star

The third star, announced at a Michelin Guide ceremony in San Diego, capped a trajectory that began with Californios earning its first star right out of the gate when it opened in 2015, according to the Associated Press. A second star followed in 2018. Cantú, with his wife Carolyn by his side, received the third star and a crisp white Michelin chef's jacket.

"It's been just really rewarding to see the team kind of like, carry a little bit more confidence in their step," Cantú told the AP. "Maybe I've got some new confidence too."

The achievement marks a first for any Mexican restaurant worldwide, according to the Associated Press. The wire service reported that the accolade feels like validation not just for Californios but for the notion that Mexican food across the diaspora can be an elevated experience.

Mexican American restaurants have dealt with criticism for charging more and offering tasting menus, the Associated Press reported, while few bat an eye at French and Japanese restaurants with similar prices. The AP noted the dynamic is similar to that of fine dining Chinese American restaurants.
