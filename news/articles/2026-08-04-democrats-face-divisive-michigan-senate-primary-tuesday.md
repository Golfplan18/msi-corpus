## DSA-aligned candidates appear on Wisconsin, Minnesota ballots next week

A divisive Democratic primary for Senate in Michigan takes place Tuesday, with the Wall Street Journal describing the contest as arguably the party's most consequential so far this year. The Journal said the race will reveal whether a progressive and socialist groundswell has spread to America's heartland.

Democrats are looking to define what they stand for with midterm elections to determine who controls Congress just three months away, according to the Journal.

Candidates aligned with the Democratic Socialists of America have notched important victories in New York and Colorado, the Journal reported, and are also on the ballot in next week's elections in Wisconsin and Minnesota.

The Journal published the description as part of a live Q&A asking readers what questions they have about the future path for the Democrats. Reporters Aaron Zitner and John McCormick will try to answer questions posted in the commenting space, on topics including the state of the party and the outlook for the 2026 midterms.

McCormick is a national political reporter who has covered every presidential campaign since 2000. Zitner, a reporter and editor in the Journal's Washington bureau, focuses on how politics are driven by demographic and economic change and also reports on trends in polling.
