Iran executed Ali Ardestani on Wednesday after state media reported his conviction for relaying sensitive information to Israel's Mossad intelligence service in exchange for cryptocurrency payments, according to the official IRNA news agency. IRNA described Ardestani as a "special operative force of Israel" and said he provided images and footage of unspecified sensitive locations to Mossad agents. His case, the agency said, was reviewed by both primary courts and the country's Supreme Court before the sentence was carried out.

The execution is the 12th Iran has carried out for espionage since an Israeli air campaign last June killed nearly 1,100 people inside the country, extending a pattern of capital sentences for alleged intelligence offenses that human rights organizations say lack procedural safeguards.

IRNA said Ardestani confessed to the charges and had hoped to receive a million-dollar reward as well as a British visa in addition to cryptocurrency payments. Israel recruited him online, according to the report. The agency did not specify when or where he was detained.

Iran has executed 12 people for espionage since the June air war, which killed nearly 1,100 people inside Iran, including senior military commanders and nuclear scientists, according to state media. Iran's retaliatory missile barrage killed 28 people in Israel. Both countries have remained on heightened alert since the exchange.

Human rights organizations and Western governments have condemned Iran's growing use of capital punishment for political and espionage-related offenses. Activists argue that many such convictions rely on coerced confessions and that trials take place behind closed doors, without access to independent legal representation.

Tehran maintains that those executed were agents of hostile intelligence services involved in acts of terrorism or sabotage. Iranian officials have accused Israel of conducting a campaign of covert operations inside Iran, including the assassination of nuclear scientists and sabotage of strategic facilities.
