## Lehigh Acres draws priced-out Florida home buyers with $310,000 median sale price

**Subtype:** fact

- Lehigh Acres, an unincorporated area east of Fort Myers, has become a destination for Florida home buyers priced out of Miami and other high-cost metros, with a median sale price of $310,000 in May, roughly half the $652,000 median in Miami.
- Teachers, police officers, and service workers priced out of coastal cities are driving demand in the exurban community, according to the Wall Street Journal.
- Federal data released this year showed the Miami metro area's cost of living has surpassed the New York metro area, pushing families toward inland areas.
- The community's last major development wave in the 2000s ran on easy credit and collapsed during the subprime mortgage crisis; the current building cycle is the largest since that bust.

## Unincorporated area relies on Lee County services as population climbs to estimated 135,000

- Lehigh Acres has no municipal government of its own and depends entirely on Lee County for services including law enforcement, road maintenance, and zoning enforcement.
- The population has grown to an estimated 135,000 residents, according to county figures, placing strain on infrastructure designed for a much smaller community.
- Rapid development has outpaced the county's ability to expand roads, drainage, and utilities, with residents reporting traffic congestion and flooding as recurring problems.
- The lack of local government means there is no city council or planning board accountable to residents; all land-use decisions flow through the county commission.
