## Jordan intercepts 10 Iranian missiles in overnight attack

Two U.S. service members were killed and one remains missing after Iranian ballistic missile and drone attacks in Jordan on Friday, the U.S. military said.

U.S. Central Command said four additional U.S. personnel were medically evacuated to hospitals in Jordan and have since been discharged. Other troops who sustained minor injuries returned to duty.

Jordan's military said it intercepted 10 Iranian missiles fired into its airspace overnight and reported no damage to its territory.

The attack occurred as the U.S. military carried out its seventh consecutive night of strikes on Iran, after President Donald Trump declared their temporary ceasefire agreement "over," according to CENTCOM.
