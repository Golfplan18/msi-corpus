## Summary

**Subtype:** fact

- Three hikers were found dead on Mount Baldy in Southern California after a report of an injured hiker prompted a rescue response amid strong winds, according to the San Bernardino County Sheriff’s Department.
- The sheriff’s department received a call reporting that a hiker had fallen 500 feet down a remote mountainside on Monday around 11:30 a.m.
- A helicopter crew spotted the injured hiker along with two other people but were unable to land due to the strong winds.
- A medic was eventually hoisted down from a helicopter around 7:30 p.m. and found the three hikers dead.
- The deaths occurred in the same wilderness area where actor Julian Sands died three years ago after going missing on a solo hike.

Rescue crews discovered the bodies of three hikers on Mount Baldy in Southern California on Monday evening, according to the San Bernardino County Sheriff’s Department. The department received a call around 11:30 a.m. reporting that a hiker had tumbled 500 feet down a remote mountainside. The caller hiked to an area with cellular service and provided GPS coordinates to rescuers, according to the AP.

A helicopter crew spotted the injured hiker and two other people, but strong winds prevented the aircraft from landing, according to the sheriff's department. A second landing attempt hours later was also unsuccessful because of the winds. A medic was eventually hoisted down from a helicopter and found three people dead around 7:30 p.m., the sheriff’s statement said.

The identities of the hikers have not been released as of Tuesday. The cause of the deaths wasn’t immediately known, according to the AP.

Southern California had been experiencing strong Santa Ana winds, with isolated gusts reaching 70 mph in some areas, the AP reported.

The deaths occurred in the wilderness near where actor Julian Sands died three years ago. Sands, who starred in “A Room With a View” and “Leaving Las Vegas,” was reported missing after setting off on a solo hike in January 2023. His body was found five months later, according to the AP.
