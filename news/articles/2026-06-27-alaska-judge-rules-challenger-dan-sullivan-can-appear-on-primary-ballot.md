- Superior Court Judge Thomas Matthews ruled Friday that Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. is eligible to appear on Alaska's August primary ballot to challenge incumbent Republican U.S. Sen. Dan Sullivan.

Superior Court Judge Thomas Matthews ruled Friday that Daniel J. Sullivan Jr., a candidate who shares the same name and party affiliation as Alaska Republican U.S. Sen. Dan Sullivan, is eligible to appear on the August primary ballot.

Matthews' decision overturns a June 15 order by Division of Elections Director Carol Beecher disqualifying the challenger and keeping him off the primary ballot.

Matthews' ruling can be appealed to the state Supreme Court.
