The driver in a crash near Lagos, Nigeria, that injured heavyweight boxer Anthony Joshua and killed two of his associates was arraigned Tuesday on charges of dangerous driving causing death and driving without a valid national driver's license. Adeniyi Mobolaji Kayode, 46, was driving Joshua and his friends in a Lexus SUV in December when the vehicle hit a stationary truck on the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway.

The driver faced serious charges including dangerous driving causing death. Joshua, a two-time heavyweight champion, returned to the United Kingdom after the crash.

The crash, which occurred in December on the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway, killed two of Joshua's close associates — Sina Ghami and Latif "Latz" Ayodele. Joshua sustained minor injuries.

## High-Speed Driving

Officials said Kayode was driving at high speed at the time of the collision.

## Charges and Bail

Kayode was initially charged with the offenses in early January. A local court granted him bail of 5 million naira, approximately $3,480, at that time.

## Court Hearing

At Tuesday's hearing before the Sagamu Magistrates Court in Ogun state, prosecutors requested an adjournment. The court approved the request and scheduled the trial for Feb. 25.

Abiodun Olalekan, Kayode's defense counsel, opposed the adjournment. "Coupled with the fact that the matter is slated for definite hearing for today, and their witnesses are in court, nothing stops the court from going on," Olalekan told reporters.

## Joshua's Nigerian Connection

Joshua, a two-time heavyweight champion, returned to the United Kingdom following the accident. He has family roots in Nigeria and briefly attended boarding school there as a child. He also holds Nigerian nationality.
