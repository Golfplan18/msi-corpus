## MAHA activists urge Trump to drop coal for AI data centers

**Subtype:** fact

- Nearly 200 activists in Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s "Make America Healthy Again" movement sent a letter Friday criticizing President Donald Trump's promotion of coal to fuel AI data centers.
- The letter, first obtained by The Associated Press, was addressed to Trump, Kennedy and other Cabinet officials and signed by at least two former staffers from Kennedy's 2024 presidential campaign.
- The letter laments a 2025 executive order aimed at boosting coal to power data centers over pollution concerns and asks the administration to consider renewables including solar and geothermal energy.
- The mobilization marks the first major MAHA movement action over fossil fuels and the latest in a series of rifts between the activists and the Trump administration over environmental policy.

## First major MAHA fossil-fuel mobilization targets data-center coal

The letter, addressed to Trump, Kennedy and various other Cabinet officials and first obtained by The Associated Press, laments a 2025 executive order that aimed to boost coal to power data centers over pollution concerns. It asks the administration to consider renewable options including solar and geothermal energy.

At least two former staffers from Kennedy's 2024 presidential campaign signed onto the letter, according to the AP.

The activists' action marks the first major MAHA movement mobilization over fossil fuels and the latest in a series of rifts between the diverse coalition and the Trump administration over environmental policy.
