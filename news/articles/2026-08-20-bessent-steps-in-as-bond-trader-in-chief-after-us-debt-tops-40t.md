## Economists view the $40 trillion threshold as largely symbolic

The Wall Street Journal's 10-Point newsletter on Thursday picked up the Treasury's Wednesday disclosure that U.S. gross debt has crossed $40 trillion for the first time. The Journal framed the development as an "eye-popping new milestone," though the same edition noted that economists view the threshold as "largely symbolic" — even as the magnitude of the figure dwarfs the entire annual output of the U.S. economy.

The newsletter placed the milestone in the context of decades of higher spending on Medicare and Social Security, tax cuts, and stimulus efforts that, taken together, have brought the U.S. to this point. The Journal tied the moment to the bond market, writing that a confluence of factors driving up bond yields has now prompted Bessent to step in as America's "bond trader in chief."
