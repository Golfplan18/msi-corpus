Authorities in Texas are asking for the public's help to find a giraffe named Gracie who escaped from a ranch in Real County.

The Real County Animal Rescue-Shelter revealed on social media that Gracie absconded from the Cedar Hollow Ranch and was last seen on a game camera west of Leakey, a small community in the Texas Hill Country.

Ranch owner Vick Jones is offering a $5,000 reward for information leading to Gracie's safe return.

Anyone who spots Gracie, described as having "rounded ears," is being asked to contact Jones or the Real County Sheriff's Office.
