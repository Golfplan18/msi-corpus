The attacks came as a U.S.-led push to negotiate an end to the conflict intensified, with Ukraine's military and European leaders scheduled to hold separate sessions in Paris this week and Zelenskyy saying work on peace proposals could now accelerate.

Ukrainian drone strikes killed two people in Russian border regions Sunday, local officials said, as President Volodymyr Zelenskyy prepared to travel to Paris for diplomatic meetings aimed at ending a war now in its fourth year.

Belgorod regional Gov. Vyacheslav Gladkov said one person died and two others — including a young child — were wounded when a Ukrainian drone struck a car. In the neighboring Kursk region, Gov. Alexander Khinshtein said one person was killed in a drone strike on a village.

## Casualties on both sides of the border

Fighting continued on the Ukrainian side of the front as well. Three people were wounded in drone strikes overnight into Sunday in Ukraine's Kharkiv region, the country's State Emergency Service said.

The death toll from a Russian missile attack on the city of Kharkiv on Friday rose to five after body parts were found under the rubble of a building, Kharkiv Mayor Ihor Terekhov said Sunday.

## Paris talks take shape

The latest strikes came as a U.S.-led diplomatic push to end the conflict intensified. National security advisers from Europe and other allies visited Kyiv on Saturday to discuss security guarantees and economic support for Ukraine, according to the Associated Press.

Zelenskyy said Saturday that work on peace proposals could now accelerate. Ukraine had shared all documents under discussion with the 18 national security advisers who attended the Kyiv meeting, including those covering security guarantees, he said.

Representatives from Ukraine's General Staff and military sector were scheduled to meet Monday in Paris. A meeting of European leaders was set for Tuesday, where Zelenskyy said he hoped documents on security guarantees would be finalized. Meetings with U.S. representatives in Paris were also planned, he said.
