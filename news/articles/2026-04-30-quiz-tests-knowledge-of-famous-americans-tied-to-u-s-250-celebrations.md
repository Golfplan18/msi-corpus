## Body

The Associated Press published a knowledge quiz centered on famous Americans connected to the U.S. 250 anniversary, asking readers to test what they know about the people included in the questions. The quiz is presented as a way to engage with notable historical figures as the country marks the 250th-year milestone.

The list of figures in the quiz includes Ronald Reagan, among other widely recognized names. It also includes Hank Aaron, Harvey Milk, and Pope Leo XIV as part of the set of featured Americans that readers are asked to identify through the quiz prompts.

The AP’s framing positions the quiz as a reader-facing test rather than a detailed explainer, with the emphasis on participation and recall. The article is filed by Holly Meyer, who leads the AP’s Global Religion team and is based in Nashville, Tennessee.

While the quiz format is designed for quick engagement, it also reflects how the U.S. 250 celebration is being used to prompt public attention to historical figures across politics, culture, and other domains. In that sense, the quiz acts as a small audience check on public memory tied to the anniversary.
