## Joyce defends One Nation super plan as Albanese labels it a threat

The Australian government confirmed on Tuesday that one citizen who had been uncontactable in Nepal has been located safe and well and does not require consular assistance. The confirmation came in a daily summary of consular activity as authorities continued efforts to account for citizens affected by the disaster. Thirty-five Australians remain missing.

The update arrived against a backdrop of substantial domestic political developments. Nationals leader Barnaby Joyce defended One Nation's superannuation policy, saying it would have a "minute" impact on inflation. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said the plan was 'a threat to the compulsory super rules,' according to the Guardian's summary. The exchange came as Guardian Australia and the ABC were again refused entry to a One Nation press conference in Victoria on the same day.
