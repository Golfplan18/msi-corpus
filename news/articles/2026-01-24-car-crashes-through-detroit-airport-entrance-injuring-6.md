A car crashed through the entrance of Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport on Friday evening, striking a ticket counter and injuring six people, according to airport officials. The driver was taken into custody, though the cause of the crash was not yet known.

## Investigation and Response

The Wayne County Airport Authority said airport police were investigating the incident. The WCAA Fire Department treated six people at the site.

## Damage Visible at the Terminal

Video posted on social media showed a blue, four-door sedan with its hood and trunk popped open, positioned in front of Delta Air Lines counters in what appeared to be a departure lobby. Glass and other debris lay strewn on the ground at the entrance, with yellow police tape cordoning off the scene.

The driver's name was not immediately released.
