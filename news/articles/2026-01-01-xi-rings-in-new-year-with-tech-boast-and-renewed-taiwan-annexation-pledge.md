BEIJING — Chinese President Xi Jinping used his New Year's Eve address to praise China's technological advances in artificial intelligence, semiconductors, and space exploration while repeating Beijing's pledge to annex Taiwan, according to a Wednesday evening broadcast by state media.

"We sought to energize high-quality development through innovation," Xi said, thanking the Chinese people for their contributions to the country's economic growth over the past five years.

Images accompanying the address showed humanoid robots performing kung fu and new hydropower projects, illustrating the technological ambitions Xi highlighted. He also cited China's rising diplomatic prominence, listing high-level political exchanges the country hosted over the past year.

China is preparing to discuss a new five-year economic development plan at the upcoming legislative session in March. The country has signaled it will accelerate self-reliance in science and technology as the United States imposes increasingly tight controls on China's access to semiconductors and other high-tech goods.

Regarding Taiwan — a self-ruled democracy that China claims as its sovereign territory — Xi reiterated Beijing's annexation intentions. As MSI previously reported when the address aired, Xi's Taiwan remarks came against the backdrop of an active military standoff in the strait: "We Chinese on both sides of the Taiwan Strait share a bond of blood and kinship," he said. "The reunification of our motherland, a trend of the times, is unstoppable." ([MSI: Xi hails China's tech gains, renews Taiwan annexation pledge](/articles/2025-12-31-xi-hails-china-s-tech-gains-renews-taiwan-annexation-pledge))

China that week conducted two days of military drills around Taiwan, launching rockets and sending aircraft and warships. Beijing said the exercises were a response to a planned U.S. arms sale to the island.

Taiwan President Lai Ching-te, in his own New Year's address on Thursday, pledged to resist China's "expansionist ambitions" and bolster the island's self-defense.
