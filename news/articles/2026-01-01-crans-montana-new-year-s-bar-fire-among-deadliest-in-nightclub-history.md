A fire at a bar in the Swiss ski resort of Crans-Montana during the early hours of New Year's Day 2026 killed approximately 40 people and left more than 100 injured, Swiss authorities said. The blaze places the Alpine resort among a documented roster of nightclub, bar, and music-venue disasters spanning more than eight decades and dozens of countries.

Recurring causes — indoor pyrotechnics, overcrowding, flammable building materials, and blocked or inadequate exits — appear across incidents separated by decades and continents, a pattern fire-safety advocates have repeatedly characterized as preventable.

## Recent incidents

**December 2025:** A fire at a popular nightclub in Arpora village, Goa, India, killed 25 people, including kitchen workers and tourists, according to the Associated Press.

**March 2025:** A fire and ensuing stampede at the crowded Pulse club in Kocani, North Macedonia, killed 63 people — most of them young revelers — and injured more than 200. The blaze was set off by a pyrotechnic flame that engulfed the club's roof.

**April 2024:** A blaze at the Masquerade nightclub in Istanbul, Turkey, killed 29 people. Workers and employees were trapped while the venue was closed for renovations; the club occupied the ground and basement floors of a 16-story residential building.

**October 2023:** A fire that started at a nightclub in Murcia, Spain, and spread to two adjacent clubs killed 13 people.

**January 2022:** A nightclub in Sorong, in Indonesia's West Papua province, burned after two groups clashed inside the building, killing 19 people.

**January 2022:** A blaze at Liv's Nightclub Yaouba in Yaoundé, Cameroon, set off explosions that killed 17 people. The government said fireworks ignited the roof and the fire spread to areas where cooking gas was stored.

## Earlier disasters

**December 2016:** Thirty-six people died in a fire at a warehouse in Oakland, California, that had been converted into an artists' residence and event space known as the Ghost Ship. The blaze broke out during an electronic music and dance party and moved quickly, trapping victims on an illegally constructed second floor.

**October 2015:** A rock band's pyrotechnics display at the Colectiv nightclub in Bucharest, Romania, ignited a blaze that killed 64 people and left approximately 190 injured.

**January 2013:** A fire killed more than 200 people at the Kiss nightclub in Santa Maria, Brazil. Investigators said soundproofing foam on the ceiling caught fire and released poisonous gases that quickly overcame those attending a university party.

**December 2009:** Approximately 152 people died when a fire broke out at the Lame Horse nightclub in Perm, Russia, after an indoor fireworks display ignited a plastic ceiling decorated with branches.

**January 2009:** An indoor fireworks display following a New Year's countdown ignited a blaze at the Santika club in Bangkok, Thailand, killing 67 people and injuring many others. Victims died from burns, smoke inhalation, and crushing.

**September 2008:** A fire killed 44 people at the King of Dancers nightclub in Shenzhen, China, after a fireworks show ignited the ceiling and a stampede broke out.

**December 2004:** A fire killed 194 people at the Cromagnon Republic club in Buenos Aires, Argentina, after a flare ignited ceiling foam. Club owner Omar Chaban was sentenced to 20 years in prison for causing the fire and for bribery; others received lighter sentences.

**February 2003:** A fire at the Station nightclub in West Warwick, Rhode Island, killed 100 people and injured more than 200 others after fireworks used by the performing band set fire to flammable foam inside the club.

**January 2001:** A fire at a café in Volendam, the Netherlands, where patrons were celebrating the New Year, killed 14 people and injured more than 200.

**December 2000:** A fire blamed on a welding accident killed 309 people at a disco in the central Chinese city of Luoyang.

**October 1998:** An arson attack against an overcrowded youth disco in Gothenburg, Sweden, killed 63 people and left approximately 200 injured. Four people were later convicted for starting the fire.

**March 1996:** A fire at the Ozone Disco Pub in Quezon City, Philippines, killed 162 people, many of them students celebrating the end of the academic year.

**March 1990:** An arson attack at the Happy Land nightclub in the Bronx, New York City, killed 87 people. The attacker poured gasoline on the club's only exit, set it alight, and jammed the metal front gate down, trapping patrons inside.

**December 1983:** A fire at the Alcala dance hall in Madrid, Spain, killed 78 people and injured more than 20.

**May 1977:** A fire at the Beverly Hills Supper Club in Southgate, Kentucky, killed 165 people and injured more than 200.

**November 1942:** The deadliest nightclub fire in United States history killed 492 people at Boston's Cocoanut Grove club, according to the Associated Press. The blaze at what had been one of Boston's foremost entertainment venues led to new requirements for sprinkler systems and accessible exits.

**April 1940:** A fire ignited decorative Spanish moss draped from the ceiling of the Rhythm Night Club in Natchez, Mississippi, killing 209 people. The windows had been boarded up to prevent people from entering without paying.
