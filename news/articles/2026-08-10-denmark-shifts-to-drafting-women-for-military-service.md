## NATO allies study gender-equal conscription as model

Denmark has shifted toward drafting women for military service, with mandatory conscription extended to 11 months from four, The Wall Street Journal reported.

The changes came amid the war in Ukraine and growing security pressures in the Arctic, according to the Journal.

NATO allies are seeking inspiration from Denmark's gender-equal conscription program, the Journal said, with the program drawing interest as a possible model for allied militaries.

The Journal joined Danish recruits as they trained, filming the program for a video report published alongside its account.
