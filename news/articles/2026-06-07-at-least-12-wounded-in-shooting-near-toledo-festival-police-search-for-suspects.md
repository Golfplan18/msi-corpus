Toledo Deputy Police Chief Joe Heffernan said at least 12 people were wounded in a shooting near the Old West End festival in Toledo, Ohio, on Saturday evening. Heffernan told reporters that two of the victims were in critical condition. Police said no arrests have been made and the search for suspects is continuing.

The shooting occurred near the Old West End festival, a neighborhood event in a historic residential district of Toledo. Heffernan provided the update in a brief press conference Saturday, the details of which were captured in a video published by The Guardian. Police have not released any information about the suspects or a possible motive.

Authorities have not said whether they believe the shooting was a targeted attack or a random act of violence. The investigation is ongoing.
