## Nissan posts first quarterly profit after two years of losses

**Subtype:** fact

- Nissan Motor Corp. recorded a net profit of 3.8 billion yen ($24 million) in the January-March quarter, reversing a 115.8 billion yen loss in the same period of 2025.
- Quarterly sales rose 9.5% to 2.96 trillion yen ($19 billion), up from 2.7 trillion yen a year earlier.
- Nissan's leadership has promised to return the company to profit in the fiscal year ending March 2027.

## Espinosa says difficulties remain in Middle East and other markets

The Yokohama-based automaker had posted losses for two consecutive fiscal years before the January-March turnaround.

Chief Executive Ivan Espinosa told reporters that cost reduction efforts were gaining momentum. He said sales were growing in the U.S. and Japan but difficulties remain in some global markets, including the Middle East.
