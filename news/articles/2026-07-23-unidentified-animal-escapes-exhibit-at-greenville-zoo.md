## Unidentified animal escapes exhibit at Greenville Zoo

**Subtype:** fact

- An unidentified animal escaped its enclosure at the Greenville Zoo in Greenville, South Carolina, on Thursday morning, the city confirmed.
- The zoo delayed its opening and Cleveland Park was closed to the public while zookeepers and police worked to contain the animal, which remained inside the zoo's walls.
- Parents of children enrolled in the zoo's Jr. Zoologist day camp received an emergency message telling them to stay away from the zoo and that dropped-off children were safe in a playground area.
- The identity of the escaped animal had not been released by the city or the zoo as of Thursday morning.

The Greenville Zoo delayed its opening Thursday morning after an unidentified animal escaped from its enclosure, according to the City of Greenville. Cleveland Park, which surrounds the zoo, was closed to the public while police and zookeepers responded.

The zoo said in a statement that it and the park were temporarily closed "while zoo personnel and first responders contain an animal that left its exhibit." The statement, provided to WYFF-TV, said the animal was inside the zoo walls and that Greenville Police and firefighters were on scene "to keep the public at a safe distance."

A parent whose child attends the zoo's day camp received a message Thursday morning that said in part: "Hello Jr. Zoologist families. We have an emergency at the Greenville Zoo. Children that have been dropped off already are in the Brook's playground. Please stay away from the zoo. We will update you as soon as possible."

The City of Greenville confirmed the zoo's opening would be delayed but did not provide additional details about the animal. The zoo had not released the identity of the escaped animal as of Thursday morning, according to United Press International.
