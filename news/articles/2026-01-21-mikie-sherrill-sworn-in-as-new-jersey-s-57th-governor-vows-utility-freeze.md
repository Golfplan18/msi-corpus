Mikie Sherrill, a Democrat and former Navy helicopter pilot, was sworn in Tuesday as New Jersey’s 57th governor in Newark, succeeding Phil Murphy. Sherrill, 54, becomes just the second woman to lead the state, according to the Associated Press.

In the inaugural remarks, Sherrill linked her political message to what she portrayed as federal actions under President Donald Trump. She said she sees “a president illegally usurping power,” and that the president “has unconstitutionally enacted a tariff regime to make billions for himself and his family.” She added that “everyone else sees costs go higher and higher,” and she said, “Here, we demand people in public service actually serve the public.”

Sherrill also used a defiant tone toward the Trump administration, saying the state would not remain quiet in the face of communities living in fear of the president’s immigration crackdown. “These are some weary years,” Sherrill said. “But I’ll be damned if in this state, at this time, we cry any silent tears.”

During the ceremony, Sherrill signed two executive orders while speaking. One order declared a freeze on utility rates that have been rising sharply. The other order was aimed at establishing new sources of electricity production, including solar and nuclear.

The AP reported that Sherrill took the oath of office on a copy of the Constitution owned by the state’s first governor. The source also described Tuesday’s ceremony as including a gun salute and a military helicopter flyover, departing from earlier inaugurations that had included similar artillery salutes along the Delaware River outside the statehouse in Trenton.

The inauguration closes Murphy’s eight-year tenure and completes the first time in more than six decades that the same political party has controlled New Jersey’s governor’s office for three straight terms, the AP said. It also noted that Sherrill won the governorship after pinning blame for high costs on the president’s tariffs and promising her first action would be to order a freeze on skyrocketing utility rates.

Murphy, in his final news conference, said he has been in regular touch with Sherrill about the transition, the AP reported. He said, “We were who we said would be,” and added, “We didn’t campaign on my thesis and pull a fast one.”

The AP said Murphy is also passing along a state budget that has swelled over the years, raising the prospect of potential shortfalls if state revenues dip. It said there is also an unfunded promise to continue a property tax relief program begun in the governor’s second term.

Sherrill will work with a Democrat-led Legislature, one of more than a dozen states where the party controls both the legislature and the governorship, the AP reported. The inauguration came as Abigail Spanberger moved into Virginia’s governor’s office, and as Democrats hope Trump will be a drag on Republican candidates in key races, according to the AP. The AP also cited Christine Todd Whitman as the prior woman to lead New Jersey, noting she later served as George W. Bush’s Environmental Protection Agency administrator.
