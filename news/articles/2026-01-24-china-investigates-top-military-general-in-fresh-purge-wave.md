The investigation signals Xi's continued effort to reshape China's military leadership through removals of top officials. The developments come as the Trump administration released a new defense strategy acknowledging China as a military competitor requiring deterrence while exploring possible pathways to acceptable peace terms.

## The Investigations

China's military announced Saturday it is investigating Zhang Youxia, the senior of two vice chairs of the Central Military Commission, for suspected serious violations of discipline and law. Liu Zhenli, chief of staff of the commission's Joint Staff Department, has also been placed under investigation by the Communist Party, according to a Defense Ministry statement. Neither the Defense Ministry nor the Communist Party provided specifics on the alleged wrongdoing.

The commission is China's top military body, and the investigations mark the latest in a series of removals at its highest levels.

## Pattern of Purges

The Communist Party expelled He Weidong, the other vice chair of the commission, last October and replaced him with Zhang Shengmin. In 2024, the party expelled two former defense ministers over corruption charges.

Analysts believe the purges serve dual purposes: institutional reform of the military and assurance that top officials remain loyal to Xi Jinping, who also chairs the military commission. The removals are part of a broader anti-corruption campaign that has punished more than 200,000 officials since Xi came to power in 2012.

## U.S. Strategic Response

The investigations came as the Trump administration released a new National Defense Strategy on Friday that acknowledges China as a military competitor requiring deterrence. While the strategy identifies China as a challenge to American interests and those of its allies, it also charts a path toward possible resolution.

"This does not require regime change or some other existential struggle," the strategy said. "Rather, a decent peace, on terms favorable to Americans but that China can also accept and live under, is possible."
