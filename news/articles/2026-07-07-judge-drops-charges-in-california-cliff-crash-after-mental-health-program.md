## Judge drops charges in California cliff crash after mental health program

**Subtype:** fact

- A San Mateo County judge dismissed all attempted murder charges against Dharmesh Patel, 45, a radiologist who drove his Tesla off a 250-foot cliff at Devil's Slide in January 2023, injuring his wife and two young children.
- Patel completed a two-year mental health diversion program supervised by a Stanford University psychiatrist and a family therapist, San Mateo County District Attorney Steve Wagstaffe said.
- Wagstaffe said the judge was required by state law to dismiss the charges once Patel finished the program.
- All four family members survived the 250-foot plunge, which one official called an "absolute miracle."

## Radiologist completes two-year diversion in Devil's Slide plunge

Prosecutors charged Patel with attempted murder after he drove his Tesla off the 250-foot (76-meter) cliff along the Pacific Coast Highway, a stretch known as Devil's Slide, on Jan. 2, 2023. The crash injured his wife and their two young children. All four survived the fall in what one official described as an "absolute miracle."

A San Mateo County judge dismissed the charges Monday after Patel completed a two-year mental health diversion program with a Stanford University psychiatrist and a family therapist this week, Wagstaffe said.

"The judge was required by the law to dismiss the charges," Wagstaffe said.

California's mental health diversion law permits certain defendants to seek treatment in lieu of prosecution. The statute requires the court to dismiss charges once a defendant who has been accepted into the program successfully completes its terms.
