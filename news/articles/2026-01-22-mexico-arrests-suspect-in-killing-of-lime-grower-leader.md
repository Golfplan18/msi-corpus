Mexican authorities arrested a man known as "El Botox" on Thursday in connection with the killing of a lime grower leader in the western state of Michoacan, according to Security Secretary Omar García Harfuch.

The suspect is César Alejandro Sepúlveda Arellano, leader of a criminal group known as the White Trojans or Blancos de Troya. García Harfuch said Arellano is allegedly responsible for extorting lime growers and for various homicides, including the killing of Bernardo Bravo, president of the Apatzingan Valley Citrus Producers Association.

Arellano faces 11 arrest orders for extortion and homicide, according to García Harfuch. He has also been accused of attacking authorities with explosives.

## Criminal Network

The White Trojans work with Los Viagras, a criminal organization allied with the Jalisco New Generation Cartel.

Michoacan Gov. Alfredo Ramírez Bedolla said the arrest represented "an overwhelming blow against extortion" in the state, which is Mexico's largest producer of limes and avocados.

## A Pattern of Violence

Bravo's body was found in his vehicle on a road in Michoacan in October. He had denounced "organized crime's permanent commercial hijacking of any commercial activity" before his death.

Two weeks after Bravo's killing, a gunman killed Uruapan Mayor Carlos Alberto Manzo. The two homicides prompted the administration of Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum to deploy additional troops to the region.
