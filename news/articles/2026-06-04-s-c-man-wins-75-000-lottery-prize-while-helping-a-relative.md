## Summary

**Subtype:** fact

- A South Carolina man won a $75,000 lottery prize after stopping to buy a scratch-off ticket while driving a relative to a doctor's appointment.
- The winning ticket was a $3 Cash Lines scratch-off purchased at an EnMarket store in Bamberg.
- The man told the South Carolina Education Lottery he had never bought a ticket at that store before.
- He described the win as "pure luck" and said he is still processing it.

A Bamberg resident who was driving a family member to a medical appointment made an unplanned stop at the EnMarket store on Main Highway in Bamberg and bought a $3 Cash Lines scratch-off ticket, according to the South Carolina Education Lottery. The ticket revealed a $75,000 prize.

The player, who did not want to be named, told lottery officials that he had never purchased a ticket from that store before. "That was pure luck," he said. "I'm still trying to process it."

Lottery officials confirmed the win and said the man exercised his right to remain anonymous under South Carolina law. The $75,000 prize is the top payout for the Cash Lines game, which costs $3 per ticket.
