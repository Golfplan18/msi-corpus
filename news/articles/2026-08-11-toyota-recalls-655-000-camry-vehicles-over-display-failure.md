## More than 508,000 U.S. Camry Hybrids are affected

Toyota Motor Corp. is recalling about 655,000 Camry vehicles worldwide, the automaker confirmed Tuesday, over a display error that may deactivate safety indicators such as turn signals and hazard lights when the cars start.

Most of the now-recalled cars were sold in the U.S., where more than 508,000 model year 2025-2026 Camry Hybrids are affected, according to documents published by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

The vehicles carry a 7-inch (17.8 centimeter) display combination meter that can become blank at startup, the NHTSA documents show. Beyond turn signals and hazard lights, certain warnings — including seat belt reminders and "key left in ignition" buzzers — may also stop functioning as a result.

NHTSA warns that the display failures increase crash risks both for drivers who may not be able to see telltale indicators and for "the ability of other road users to recognize the driver's intent to change direction or indicate a vehicle hazard."
