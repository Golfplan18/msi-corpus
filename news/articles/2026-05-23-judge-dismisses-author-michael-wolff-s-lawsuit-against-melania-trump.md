## Summary

**Subtype:** fact

- A federal judge dismissed author Michael Wolff’s lawsuit against first lady Melania Trump over statements Wolff says caused reputational and financial harm.
- Judge Mary Kay Vyskocil said Wolff and Trump “have a real dispute,” but must litigate “according to the same procedures as everyone else.”
- Vyskocil rejected Wolff’s request that the court declare he did not defame Trump and address potential liability for any later suit.
- Melania Trump’s lawyer, Alejandro Brito, sent Wolff a letter warning Trump would be “left with no alternative” but to sue if he did not retract statements.
- In April, Melania Trump denied any affiliation with Jeffrey Epstein in a statement at the White House, which her camp said responded to “unfound and baseless lies.”
