The acquisition would consolidate two of the country's smaller leisure-focused carriers into a single operator with broader geographic reach, pending approval from federal regulators and Sun Country shareholders — a process the companies expect to complete in the second half of 2026.

LAS VEGAS — Allegiant Air said Monday it will acquire Sun Country Airlines in a cash-and-stock deal valued at about $1.5 billion, including debt, combining two budget U.S. carriers that focus on leisure travel.

The deal, which still requires approval from federal regulators and Sun Country shareholders, would create an airline serving approximately 175 cities across more than 650 routes with a fleet of roughly 195 aircraft. The companies expect the transaction to close in the second half of 2026.

Allegiant CEO Gregory Anderson said the carriers' route networks complement each other and that combining them would expand affordable travel options.

"Allegiant and Sun Country have both shown that our leisure-focused, flexible capacity models are strong, thriving and consistently profitable, which gives me great confidence in the potential benefits of combining our organizations," Anderson said.

## Leadership and headquarters

The merged carrier will operate under the Allegiant name and be headquartered in Las Vegas. Anderson will lead the combined airline as CEO.

Sun Country CEO Jude Bricker will join the company's board of directors. Bricker previously served as Allegiant's chief operating officer in 2016 and 2017 before becoming Sun Country's top executive.

"I've had the privilege of working at both companies and can say that based on those experiences, this is a tremendous fit across the board," Bricker said.

## What travelers can expect

The airlines said passengers should not expect any immediate changes. Ticketing, flight schedules, the overall travel experience, and the Sun Country brand will remain the same while the merger is pending, the companies said.

The combined carrier will maintain a significant presence in the Minneapolis–St. Paul area, where Sun Country is based, and will continue to operate Sun Country's charter and cargo businesses.
