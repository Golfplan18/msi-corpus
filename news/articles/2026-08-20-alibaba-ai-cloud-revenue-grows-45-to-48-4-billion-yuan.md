## Alibaba AI cloud revenue grows 45% to 48.4 billion yuan

**Subtype:** fact

- Alibaba's AI cloud and compute services revenue grew 45% to 48.4 billion yuan ($7.2 billion) in the April-June quarter.
- Capital expenditures grew 75% to 67.7 billion yuan (about $10 billion), driven by investments in AI infrastructure to meet customer demand.
- Total quarterly revenue grew 9% to nearly 269 billion yuan ($40 billion).
- Net profit fell 75% to 10.5 billion yuan ($1.6 billion) from 43.1 billion yuan ($6.4 billion) a year earlier.

## Quarterly profit fell 75% as capital spending grew

Alibaba Group reported earnings on Thursday showing a 75% drop in quarterly profit alongside a 45% increase in revenue from its AI cloud and compute services.

The Hong Kong- and U.S.-listed company said its profit for the April-June quarter was 10.5 billion yuan ($1.6 billion), down from 43.1 billion yuan ($6.4 billion) in the same quarter a year earlier.

Total quarterly revenue grew 9% to nearly 269 billion yuan (about $40 billion). Revenue from AI cloud and compute services rose 45% to 48.4 billion yuan ($7.2 billion) during the quarter.

Capital expenditures, including investments in AI infrastructure to meet customer demand, grew 75% to 67.7 billion yuan (about $10 billion) during the quarter.

Alibaba, which started in e-commerce and online retail, is increasingly focused on AI technologies.
