Hong Kong ushered in 2026 without fireworks Wednesday night, replacing its signature Victoria Harbor pyrotechnic display with a music concert and landmark light show after a November apartment fire killed at least 161 people — the city's worst blaze since 1948.

The cancellation marks the latest in a series of holiday fireworks displays scrapped following mass-casualty events in Hong Kong, and comes as thousands of residents displaced by the Wang Fuk Court fire continue to recover in transitional housing.

The city's tourism board hosted a music show in Central, the business district adjoining the Lan Kwai Fong nightlife hub, featuring soft rock duo Air Supply and other performers. At midnight, the facades of eight landmarks transformed into giant countdown clocks displaying a three-minute light show.

Rosanna Law, the territory's secretary for culture, sports and tourism, acknowledged Tuesday that having no fireworks would affect some hotel and restaurant businesses.

Tourists expressed understanding. Annie Wang, a university student visiting from Shanghai, said she had planned to watch the fireworks show but accepted the decision.

"It's quite regretful. But there's no way around it after the fire," Wang said.

Wang Miao, a teacher from the neighboring city of Guangzhou, said the absence of pyrotechnics would not diminish her visit.

"It doesn't affect our experience in Hong Kong," Wang said.

## The November fire

The Wang Fuk Court fire — [as MSI previously reported](/articles/2025-12-31-hong-kong-greets-2026-without-fireworks-after-deadliest-blaze-in-decades) — broke out in the northern suburban district of Tai Po in late November at an apartment complex undergoing monthslong renovation. The buildings were covered by bamboo scaffolding and green netting at the time. Authorities have pointed to substandard netting and foam boards installed on windows as contributing factors in the fire's rapid spread. Thousands of affected residents have since moved to transitional homes, hotels and youth hostels, the Associated Press reported.

## A pattern of cancellations

Hong Kong has cancelled fireworks following previous mass-casualty events. A vessel collision that killed 39 people on Oct. 1, 2012, led to the cancellation of the 2013 National Day fireworks. A bus crash that left 19 dead prompted the scrapping of the 2018 Lunar New Year display. Multiple displays were also cancelled during the 2019 anti-government protests and the COVID-19 pandemic.

Pyrotechnic displays against Hong Kong's skyline of skyscrapers typically draw hundreds of thousands of people, including many tourists, to both sides of the Victoria Harbor promenade.
