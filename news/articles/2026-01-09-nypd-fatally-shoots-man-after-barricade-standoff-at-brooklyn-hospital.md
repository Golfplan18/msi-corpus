New York City police fatally shot a man wielding a sharp object who had barricaded himself inside a room at NewYork-Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist Hospital on Thursday, trapping an elderly patient and a hospital security worker with him, the New York City Police Department said.

The incident occurred around 5:30 p.m. on the eighth floor of the hospital, located in Brooklyn's Park Slope neighborhood, Assistant Chief Charles Minch said.

Officers arriving at the scene found the man — who had been admitted as a patient the day before — in a blood-spattered room. He had barricaded himself inside with an older adult patient and a hospital security worker, Minch said, and threatened to harm both himself and others.

For more than three minutes, officers repeatedly ordered the man to drop the sharp object and open the door. He refused, Minch said.

Officers deployed Tasers twice in an attempt to subdue him; both efforts failed. They also tried to kick the door open, but the man held it shut, Minch said.

After the second Taser attempt failed, the man charged at the officers with the object. Officers then opened fire, Minch said.

"This situation could have turned out every [sic] differently," Minch said.

The department posted a photograph of a bloodied "self-made" weapon on social media following the shooting. Officers' body cameras recorded the incident, Minch added.

The man was not publicly identified. The hospital security worker was evaluated by medical personnel after the incident, though the extent of any injuries was not immediately clear, according to police.
