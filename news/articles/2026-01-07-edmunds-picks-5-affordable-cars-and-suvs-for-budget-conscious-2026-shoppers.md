Automotive research firm Edmunds ranked five vehicles it says offer the best combination of price, fuel economy, and safety for 2026 car shoppers, spanning compact sedans and three-row SUVs with base prices from $25,790 to $41,035. The rankings, authored by Edmunds contributor Christian Wardlaw and distributed by the Associated Press on January 7, weigh EPA fuel-economy estimates and crash-test ratings alongside sticker price.

With consumer budgets under pressure entering the new year, Edmunds frames vehicle affordability as a central concern for new-car shoppers — and argues that buying within a tight budget does not require accepting a lesser vehicle.

"Choosing an affordable vehicle doesn't mean settling for second-best," Edmunds said in the guide.

## Compact Car: Honda Civic LX

Edmunds ranked the 2026 Honda Civic LX as its top pick for affordable compact transportation, starting at $25,790. A 150-horsepower four-cylinder engine powers the sedan, with an EPA-estimated 36 mpg in combined city and highway driving. The Civic earns favorable crash-test ratings, Edmunds said.

Buyers willing to spend more can choose hybrid and performance variants. The Civic Hybrid achieves up to 49 mpg, Edmunds said, while driving enthusiasts can opt for the Si sedan or Type R hatchback.

## Midsize Sedan: Toyota Camry

The 2026 Toyota Camry, starting at $30,195, is the only model on the Edmunds list in which every trim level comes standard with a hybrid powertrain. The EPA estimates fuel economy at 43 to 51 mpg depending on trim, and all-wheel drive is available for buyers in snowy climates, Edmunds said.

## Small SUV: Hyundai Kona

At $26,950 to start, the 2026 Hyundai Kona is Edmunds' pick in the small crossover SUV segment. The EPA estimates the Kona averages 26 to 31 mpg depending on trim level and drivetrain. An electric version is also available, and the model carries a long warranty and ownership perks Edmunds said contributed to its value.

## Compact SUV: Kia Sportage Hybrid

Buyers who need more interior room than the Kona provides can step up to the 2026 Kia Sportage Hybrid, which starts at $31,735. Its turbocharged hybrid powertrain returns an EPA-estimated 42 mpg combined, or 35 mpg with all-wheel drive, Edmunds said. Kia updated the model for 2026 with design changes and added new S and X-Line trim levels.

## Midsize Three-Row SUV: Hyundai Palisade

The redesigned 2026 Hyundai Palisade tops the Edmunds list at $41,035 to start. The three-row SUV seats up to eight people, and an available turbocharged hybrid powertrain carries an EPA-estimated 34 mpg, Edmunds said. Edmunds characterized the top-trim Palisade Calligraphy as a budget alternative to luxury brands including Acura and Volvo.
