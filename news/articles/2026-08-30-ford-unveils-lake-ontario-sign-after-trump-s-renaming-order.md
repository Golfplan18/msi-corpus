## Trade talks between US and Canada collapsed last week

Ontario Premier Doug Ford installed a billboard-sized "Lake Ontario" sign on the lake's shoreline, responding to a Thursday executive order in which President Donald Trump directed US federal agencies to rename the body of water "Lake America" in US maps, documents, and databases.

The order directs US federal agencies to change the lake's name on US maps, documents, and databases to "Lake America."

Ford told reporters that the lake's name, which is more than 400 years old, would remain unchanged.
