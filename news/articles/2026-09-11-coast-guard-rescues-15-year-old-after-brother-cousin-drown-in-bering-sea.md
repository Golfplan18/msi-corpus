## Three aboard 18-foot fishing vessel departed St. Lawrence Island September 4

A 15-year-old boy was rescued by the U.S. Coast Guard on September 10 after spending days on a capsized 18-foot fishing vessel in the Bering Sea, according to a Coast Guard statement. His older brother and cousin drowned when the boat overturned.

The three had last been seen departing St. Lawrence Island, Alaska, on September 4 in the small fishing vessel, the Coast Guard said. The group was expected to return early on Sunday but did not arrive as scheduled.

The vessel capsized days after the September 4 departure. The teen was recovered from the overturned boat on September 10, several days after the capsizing.

Available accounts of the Coast Guard's initial statement did not detail the circumstances of the capsizing or how the teen was located after days on the disabled vessel.
