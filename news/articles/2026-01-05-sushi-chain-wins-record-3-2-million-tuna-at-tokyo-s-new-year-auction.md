TOKYO — A 535-pound bluefin tuna sold for a record $3.2 million at Tokyo's Toyosu fish market on Monday, setting a new high at Japan's celebrated annual New Year's auction.

Kiyomura Corp., whose owner Kiyoshi Kimura runs the popular Sushi Zanmai chain, placed the winning bid at the predawn event. The prize fish — weighing 243 kilograms and fetching 510 million yen — was caught off the coast of Oma in northern Japan, a region known for producing some of the country's finest bluefin tuna.

Hundreds of tuna change hands each day at Toyosu's early morning sessions. The first auction of the New Year draws sharply elevated prices above the daily norm, particularly for coveted Oma fish, according to the Associated Press.

Bidders walked among rows of massive tuna laid out on the market floor before sunrise. Associated Press photographer Louise Delmotte documented the event, moving through the market before dawn to capture the scene.
