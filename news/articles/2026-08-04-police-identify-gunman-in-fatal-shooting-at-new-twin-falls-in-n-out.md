## Police identify gunman in fatal shooting at new Twin Falls In-N-Out

**Subtype:** fact

- Police identified 24-year-old Chad Williams as the gunman who killed three people Saturday at an In-N-Out Burger in Twin Falls, Idaho, before fatally shooting himself.
- Ashley Garibay, an In-N-Out employee from Stockton, California, was among those killed; she had traveled to Idaho to help expand the restaurant chain.
- Her family called her an "amazing, beautiful soul who loved living life."
- Twin Falls police said they are confident Williams acted alone but have not disclosed what motivated the shooting.

## Stockton employee had traveled to Idaho to train new workers

The shooting occurred Saturday afternoon at an In-N-Out Burger in Twin Falls. Garibay had been based in Stockton and was tapped to train new employees away from Stockton for the chain's expansion. Her family said she loved her work.

In a statement Tuesday, her family described her as "adventurous, outgoing, smart and driven, never letting obstacles get in her way." They added that "she lit up a room with her bright smile and a laugh that was contagious."

Twin Falls police Lt. Steven Gassert said Tuesday that more details were expected later in the day. He did not elaborate.
