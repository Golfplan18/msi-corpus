## Summary

**Subtype:** fact

- Winnabow residents Jason Lowry and Marci Lowry won a $25,000-a-year-for-life prize on the Lucky for Life lottery after matching all five white balls in the Dec. 27 drawing.
- The siblings bought their $2 ticket at the E-Z Way Grocery on North Howe Street in Southport, North Carolina.
- They selected numbers representing family birthdays, a strategy they told lottery officials they use regularly.
- The Lowrys said the annuity will be used to pay bills and fund car and home repairs.

Brother and sister Jason Lowry and Marci Lowry, residents of Winnabow, North Carolina, purchased their $2 Lucky for Life ticket from the E-Z Way Grocery on North Howe Street in Southport, according to the North Carolina Education Lottery.

The siblings told lottery officials they selected a series of numbers corresponding to family birthdays for the drawing. Their selections matched all five white balls in the Dec. 27 drawing, earning each of them the $25,000-a-year-for-life annuity.

"We are thrilled that the winning numbers represent special dates," the siblings said in a statement provided by the lottery.

Jason Lowry said the prize money would go toward covering household costs. "This is really sweet," he told lottery officials. The siblings said they plan to use the winnings to pay bills and to fund car and home repairs.
