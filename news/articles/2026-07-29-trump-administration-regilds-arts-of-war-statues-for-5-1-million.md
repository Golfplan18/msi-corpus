## Trump administration regilds Arts of War statues for $5.1 million

**Subtype:** fact

- The Trump administration regilded the Arts of War statues near the Lincoln Memorial with 23.75-karat gold, at a cost of $5.1 million, according to federal records.
- BBC News reported on the project and interviewed visitors in Washington about their reaction to the renovation.
- The project's cost and the purity of the gold are documented in federal records.

The Trump administration has regilded the Arts of War statues near the Lincoln Memorial in Washington DC with 23.75-karat gold, according to federal records cited by BBC News. The project cost $5.1 million, the records show.

A video report by BBC News featured interviews with people in Washington who were asked for their reactions to the renovated statues. The report did not identify any of the visitors by name. BBC News described the statues as being located near the Lincoln Memorial. The equestrian sculptures are known as the Arts of War statues.
