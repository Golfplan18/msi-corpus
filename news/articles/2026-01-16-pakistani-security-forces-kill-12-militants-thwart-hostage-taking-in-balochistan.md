Pakistani security forces killed at least 12 militants and thwarted an attempted hostage-taking in Kharan district of Balochistan province on Thursday, the military said. Assailants attacked a police station and two banks, looting millions of rupees before being forced to retreat.

The attackers attempted to seize hostages at the police station, but security forces responded and drove them back, according to a military statement. The assailants also targeted two banks in Kharan and looted millions of rupees during the assault, the statement said.

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi praised the security forces for killing the militants and foiling the attacks.

Pakistan's military identified the assailants as "Fitna al-Hindustan," the government's term for the outlawed Baloch Liberation Army, or BLA, and allied separatist groups. The military claimed the attackers were backed by India, but provided no evidence for that assertion. India has repeatedly denied Pakistan's accusations that it supports separatists in Balochistan or Pakistani Taliban fighters.

The BLA, which the United States designated a terrorist organization in 2019, has carried out numerous attacks targeting security forces and civilians across Balochistan in recent years.

Balochistan, Pakistan's largest but least populated province, has long been the scene of insurgency by separatist groups, as well as attacks by the Pakistani Taliban.
