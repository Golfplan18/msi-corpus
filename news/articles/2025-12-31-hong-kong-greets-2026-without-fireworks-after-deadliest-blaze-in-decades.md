HONG KONG — Hong Kong welcomed 2026 without fireworks Wednesday night, replacing its traditional pyrotechnic display over Victoria Harbor with a music concert and a landmark light show after a late-November apartment fire killed at least 161 people — the territory's deadliest blaze since 1948.

The cancellation marks the most significant dimming of Hong Kong's signature New Year celebrations in recent memory, reflecting the weight of a mass-casualty disaster that displaced thousands of residents and left a city still in mourning weeks later.

## A different kind of countdown

The territory's tourism board hosted a music show in Central, a business district also home to the Lan Kwai Fong nightlife hub, featuring soft-rock duo Air Supply and other singers. At midnight, facades of eight landmarks transformed into giant countdown clocks and displayed a three-minute light show.

By early Wednesday evening, crowds of revelers had already gathered near the performance stage seeking good views of the musical performance.

Rosanna Law, Hong Kong's secretary for culture, sports and tourism, acknowledged Tuesday that the absence of fireworks would affect some hotel and restaurant businesses.

Visitors to the city said they understood the decision. "It's quite regretful. But there's no way around it after the fire," said Annie Wang, a university student from Shanghai who had planned to watch the fireworks. Wang Miao, a teacher from the neighboring city of Guangzhou, planned to join the official countdown activities in Central despite the change. "It doesn't affect our experience in Hong Kong," Wang said.

## The fire that changed the holiday

The blaze broke out at Wang Fuk Court, an apartment complex in the northern suburban district of Tai Po, in late November. The complex was undergoing a monthslong renovation project, its buildings covered by bamboo scaffolding and green netting. Authorities have pointed to substandard netting and foam boards installed on windows as contributing factors in the fire's rapid spread.

Thousands of residents displaced by the disaster have moved to transitional homes, hotels and youth hostels as they struggle to recover from the loss of lives and homes that had taken years to acquire.

## A recurring pattern

Hong Kong has canceled major fireworks displays before in response to mass-casualty events. Authorities called off the 2013 National Day fireworks after a vessel collision killed 39 people on Oct. 1, 2012, and suspended the 2018 Lunar New Year display after a bus crash left 19 dead. Fireworks were also scrapped during the 2019 anti-government protests and at points during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Fireworks have long been central to Hong Kong's celebrations for the New Year, Lunar New Year and National Day, with pyrotechnic displays against the city's skyline of skyscrapers typically drawing hundreds of thousands of spectators — including many tourists — to both sides of the harbor promenade.
