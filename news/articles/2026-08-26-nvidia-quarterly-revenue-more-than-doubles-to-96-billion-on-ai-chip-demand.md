## Nvidia quarterly revenue more than doubles to $96 billion on AI chip demand

- Nvidia reported quarterly revenue of $96.22 billion for the May-July period, more than doubling from a year earlier and surpassing analyst forecasts.
- Nvidia's net income reached $59.69 billion, up from $26.42 billion in the same quarter last year.

## Net income tops $59 billion, more than doubling year-ago figure

Nvidia on Wednesday reported net income of $59.69 billion, or $2.46 per share, for the May-July period. That compares to net income of $26.42 billion, or $1.08 per share, in the same quarter last year.

The Santa Clara, California-based company's revenue more than doubled from a year earlier to $96.22 billion, driven by sales of its high-end artificial intelligence chips. The result surpassed analysts' average forecast of $92.27 billion.

On an adjusted basis, excluding certain items, earnings came in at $2.22 per share, well above the $2.09 per share consensus compiled by Wall Street analysts via FactSet.

The latest quarter extends a three-year stretch in which Nvidia has regularly cleared the bar set by analysts, often by a wide margin. That streak dates to the period when the company's high-end chips emerged as artificial intelligence's best building blocks.

The results represent the latest indication that spending on AI infrastructure remains strong.
