Gerber is recalling certain lots of its arrowroot biscuits that might contain pieces of soft plastic or paper, the company said.

The recall includes 5.5-ounce packages of the biscuits, which are often given to teething babies, with best-by dates between Oct. 16 and Dec. 16, 2026.

Gerber said the plastic and paper pieces came from a supplier of arrowroot flour, which initiated the recall.

The company said no illnesses or injuries have been reported.

Consumers are advised to check the back of the packages for the codes of the affected lots. Gerber said the products can be returned to the place of purchase for a refund.

Gerber said no other Gerber products are affected by the recall.
