## Talarico courts border Hispanic voters in Texas Senate race

**Subtype:** fact

- Democratic Senate candidate James Talarico is touring border communities in Texas, stopping at Memo's Restaurant in Eagle Pass, as part of a campaign swing through predominantly Hispanic counties.
- Local resident Bibi Valdez, who attended the Eagle Pass event, told the Associated Press she was puzzled by the shift of Hispanic voters toward Republicans in the 2024 election.

## Talarico seeks support from Hispanic voters along the Texas border

EAGLE PASS, Texas — The black campaign bus with "Talarico for Texas" across its side pulled into a stop at Memo's Restaurant along the U.S.-Mexico border this month, where Bibi Valdez said she was puzzled by the political shift she had witnessed in her community.

"A lot of people, for some reason, especially the Hispanics, changed to Republican," Valdez said as security guards created space for James Talarico, the Democratic U.S. Senate candidate. "I just don't see what the logic was."

Talarico is on a quest to become the first Democrat to win a statewide election in Texas since 1994, according to the Associated Press. Memo's Restaurant was one stop on a winding trip along the border where he is searching for support from predominantly Hispanic counties that became national political curiosities after the 2024 election, the AP reported.
