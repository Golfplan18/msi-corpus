The strike added to a death toll that has climbed steadily since a ceasefire took effect in Gaza in October 2025, with the Gaza Health Ministry reporting more than 400 people killed at hospitals and another 684 found dead in rubble during that period—even as Israel said the strike on Monday complied with the ceasefire agreement.

An Israeli airstrike struck a tent housing displaced people in the southern Gaza Strip on Monday, killing a 5-year-old girl and her uncle and wounding two other children, according to officials at Nasser Hospital. The strike hit the Muwasi area northwest of Khan Younis. The Associated Press said it could not independently verify those details.

Family members wept over the bodies as they were brought to the hospital, the Associated Press reported.

The Israeli military said on social media it had struck a Hamas militant who planned an imminent attack on Israeli troops in the southern Gaza Strip. The military said the strike complied with the ceasefire agreement and was conducted in a targeted way to limit civilian harm. It was not immediately clear whether the statement referred to the fatal tent strike.

The two dead were among the more than 400 people killed in Gaza since an October 2025 ceasefire took effect, according to the Gaza Health Ministry. The ministry said 422 bodies had been brought to hospitals since the ceasefire went into effect on Oct. 10, and that another 684 people had been found dead in rubble across Gaza during the same period. An additional 1,189 people were wounded since the ceasefire began, the ministry said. The ministry, part of the Hamas-run government, maintains detailed casualty records that United Nations agencies and independent experts consider generally reliable, the Associated Press reported.

The overall Palestinian death toll from the Israel-Hamas war rose to at least 71,388, with 171,269 wounded, the ministry said.

Also on Monday, the Israeli military said it had begun striking Hezbollah and Hamas targets in southern and eastern Lebanon, citing continued ceasefire violations. Israel and Hezbollah had exchanged fire for more than a year before reaching a ceasefire in November 2024. Israel continues to hold small parts of Lebanese territory and has continued to carry out strikes there, accusing Hezbollah of attempting to rearm.

The Lebanon strikes came a few days before Lebanon's army commander was scheduled to brief the government on the mission of disarming Hezbollah in areas along the border with Israel, the Associated Press reported.
