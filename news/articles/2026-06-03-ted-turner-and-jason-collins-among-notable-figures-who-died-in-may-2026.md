## Summary

- Ted Turner, the founder of CNN who introduced the 24-hour news cycle, died in May 2026 at age 87.
- Jason Collins, who became the first openly gay player in the NBA in 2013 and later an inclusion advocate, died in May 2026.

Ted Turner, a brash and outspoken media mogul, gave the world the 24-hour news cycle when he launched CNN, according to the Associated Press. His media empire grew to include TNT, the Cartoon Network, and Turner Classic Movies. Turner also owned the Atlanta Braves Major League Baseball team and was a philanthropist and conservationist.

Jason Collins spent 13 seasons in the NBA across six franchises. In 2013, he revealed he was gay, becoming the first openly gay player in the league, the AP reported. The announcement came near the end of his playing career. Collins went on to become an advocate for inclusion. Shortly before his death, he received the inaugural Bill Walton Global Champion Award at the Green Sports Alliance Summit but was too ill to attend; his twin brother, former NBA player Jarron Collins, accepted on his behalf.
