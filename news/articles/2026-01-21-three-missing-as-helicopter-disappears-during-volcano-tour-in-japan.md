Rescuers spotted wreckage believed to be from a sightseeing helicopter carrying three people that went missing near Mount Aso in southern Japan, authorities said Wednesday. The helicopter lost contact Monday during what should have been a 10-minute tour of the volcano in Kumamoto prefecture. Strong winds and volcanic gases have hampered rescue efforts at the scene.

Mount Aso is one of Japan's most active volcanoes and a popular destination for sightseeing helicopter tours. The incident marks the second significant problem involving Takumi Enterprise's helicopter operations at the volcano.

The pilot and two passengers aboard the aircraft, operated by Takumi Enterprise, remain unaccounted for. Takumi Morioka, the operator's head, said during a news conference that he hopes the three people survived. Local police said the wreckage was spotted inside the crater of Nakadake, one of Mount Aso's five peaks, on Tuesday.

## Prior Incidents at Mount Aso

The incident marks the second significant problem involving Takumi Enterprise's helicopter operations at Mount Aso. Last year, a helicopter operated by the company made an emergency landing while flying over the volcano, injuring three people on board.

## About Mount Aso

Mount Aso is one of Japan's most active volcanoes. It last erupted in October 2021. Volcano viewing helicopter tours around Mount Aso are popular attractions in the Kumamoto prefecture in southwestern Japan.
