CORTINA D'AMPEZZO, Italy — A 55-year-old security guard died during a subfreezing overnight shift at a construction site near a 2026 Winter Olympic venue in this mountain resort, Italian authorities confirmed Saturday. Temperatures dropped to minus 12 degrees Celsius (10.4 degrees Fahrenheit) during the shift, according to Italian media reports.

The death, which Italian media reported occurred Thursday night at a site adjacent to Cortina's ice arena, raised questions about worker safety at Olympic construction sites less than four weeks before the Milan Cortina Winter Games are set to open. Italy's Infrastructure Minister Matteo Salvini called for a full investigation into the circumstances.

Officials at the Fondazione Milano Cortina 2026 attributed the death to natural causes. "The information we have is that it was a death by natural cause, it was a heart attack. And we are investigating," Andrea Varnier, CEO of the foundation, told reporters Saturday at a test event at the new hockey arena in Milan.

Varnier said site documentation was in order and that emergency services had characterized the death as occurring from natural causes while the worker was on duty.

"All the documentation that we have was in order. And we are waiting for the investigation to understand what the specific cause was. At the moment, the information we have from the emergency services is it was a death caused by natural causes ... while he was on site," Varnier said.

Simico, the governmental company responsible for overseeing Olympic infrastructure, said the construction site was not under its supervision. The company issued a statement expressing its condolences.

Cortina city officials said they were "deeply saddened and troubled by the death."

The Milan Cortina Winter Olympics are scheduled for Feb. 6–22. Cortina will host curling, sliding and women's Alpine skiing events.
