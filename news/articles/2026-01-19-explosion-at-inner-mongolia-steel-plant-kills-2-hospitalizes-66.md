An explosion at a Baogang United Steel plant in the city of Baotou, in China's Inner Mongolia region, killed two people and left 66 others hospitalized Sunday afternoon, according to China's official news agency Xinhua. Three of those hospitalized suffered serious injuries, and five people remained missing, Xinhua reported. Rescue crews rushed to the scene following the blast, which sent heavy plumes of smoke into the sky and caused tremors in surrounding areas.

Authorities were still investigating the cause of the explosion, which struck around 3 p.m. local time. Baogang United Steel is a major state-linked steel producer based in Baotou.

The Associated Press, citing Xinhua, reported that rescue operations were under way as of Sunday evening. No further details about the identities of those killed or the condition of those missing were immediately available.
