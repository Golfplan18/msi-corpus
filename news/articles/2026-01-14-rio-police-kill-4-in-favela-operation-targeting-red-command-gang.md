Rio de Janeiro's police killed four people Wednesday during an operation in the Salgueiro favela complex aimed at containing the advance of the Red Command criminal gang, state officials said. Officers also seized weapons, drugs and communications equipment during the raid. A police helicopter flying near Salgueiro was struck by gunfire in a separate incident, leaving one crew member injured.

The state's civil police said it had been verifying intelligence and strengthening ongoing investigations in the Salgueiro complex, which lies across the bay from Rio, when the confrontation occurred.

"During the operation, four criminals attacked the agents and were neutralized in a confrontation," police said in a statement.

## Weapons and equipment seized

Officers recovered four rifles, six pistols, one revolver, two replica pistols, a large quantity of ammunition, drugs, camouflage clothing and radios, police said.

## Helicopter struck

A police helicopter was hit as it flew over an area near Salgueiro, injuring one person aboard, police said in a separate statement.

The civil police said attacks on its armored aircraft have drastically increased in recent years, describing such actions as the "modus operandi of criminal factions, which open fire to prevent the presence of security forces near communities."

In its statement, the institution said it aims to "combat these narco-terrorists" — a term police used referencing language employed by U.S. President Donald Trump to designate drug traffickers.
