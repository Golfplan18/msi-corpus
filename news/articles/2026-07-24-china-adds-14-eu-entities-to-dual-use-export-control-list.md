## China adds 14 EU entities to dual-use export control list

**Subtype:** fact

- China's Commerce Ministry added 14 European entities to an export control list on July 24, banning the export of dual-use items to those organizations.
- A Chinese Commerce Ministry spokesperson said the measures were taken in retaliation for the EU's decision on Thursday to blacklist 14 mainland Chinese and Hong Kong enterprises as part of its latest Russia sanctions.
- The affected European companies include Czech vehicle manufacturer Tatra Trucks, Italian electric motor maker Lafert SpA, German manufacturer Sindlhauser Materials GmbH, and French drone manufacturer Cavok UAS.
- The order also bars foreign companies from providing dual-use items made in China to the listed entities.

## EU blacklisted 14 Chinese enterprises day before in Russia sanctions

The Commerce Ministry's statement said Chinese companies will not be allowed to export dual-use items — which can be used for both civilian and military purposes — to the 14 European organizations. The order also prohibits foreign companies from supplying the blacklisted entities with dual-use items manufactured in China.

The affected European companies include Czech vehicle manufacturer Tatra Trucks, Italian electric motor maker Lafert SpA, German manufacturer Sindlhauser Materials GmbH, and French drone manufacturer Cavok UAS.

A Chinese Commerce Ministry spokesperson said the measures were taken in retaliation for the EU on Thursday adding 14 mainland Chinese and Hong Kong enterprises to its latest list of sanctions against Russia over its war in Ukraine.
