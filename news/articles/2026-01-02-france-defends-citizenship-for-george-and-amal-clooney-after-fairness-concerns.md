George Clooney and his wife Amal Clooney were granted French citizenship, the French government said Wednesday, defending the naturalization after a junior minister questioned the fairness of the decision.

The French government said the couple were awarded citizenship because “they contribute, through their distinguished actions, to France’s international influence and cultural outreach,” according to the report.

The Clooneys’ naturalizations, along with those of their twins Ella and Alexander, were announced last weekend in France’s Journal Officiel, where government decrees are published.

Marie-Pierre Vedrenne, a junior minister at the Interior Ministry, expressed misgivings in an interview with broadcaster France Info, saying the arrangement could lead some of the Clooneys’ new French compatriots to believe they received special treatment. Vedrenne said: “The message being sent is not good,” and added, “There is an issue of fairness that, in my eyes, is absolutely essential.”

The Foreign Ministry said the Clooneys were eligible under a French law that allows naturalization for foreign nationals who contribute to France’s international influence and economic well-being. It argued that France’s cinema industry would benefit from George Clooney’s clout as a global movie star, and said Amal Clooney, a lawyer, regularly works with academic institutions and international organizations in France.

The Foreign Ministry also said “they maintain strong personal, professional and family ties with our country,” and said it was “delighted to welcome Georges and Amal Clooney into the national community,” using a French form of George’s first name.

The decision was defended by Laurent Nuñez, the Interior Minister, who said he signed the naturalization decree. Nuñez said: “It’s a big chance for our country.”

The report said the Clooneys purchased an estate in France in 2021, and Clooney has said it is their primary residence. It was not clear from the report whether Clooney retained American citizenship.

French media have reported that the Clooneys live part-time at a luxury 18th-century villa outside Brignoles in southern France, and that French privacy laws help protect their children from unauthorized photographs.

The report also said Clooney has publicly described trying to learn French using a language-learning app, and noted that in recent media interviews he joked that his wife and children speak French in front of him so they can say “horrible, horrible” things to him.

In an Esquire interview in October, Clooney said he worried about raising their kids in Los Angeles in the culture of Hollywood, adding that he did not want them to be “walking around worried about paparazzi” and did not want them compared to other famous children. He said that growing up away from the spotlight in France means “they have dinner with grown-ups and have to take their dishes in,” and that “they have a much better life.”
