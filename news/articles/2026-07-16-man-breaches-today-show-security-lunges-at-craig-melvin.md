## Man breaches Today show security, lunges at Craig Melvin

**Subtype:** fact

- A man breached security at NBC's "Today" show studios in New York City on Thursday morning, lunged at co-host Craig Melvin and shouted a racial slur before being taken into custody, the New York Police Department said.
- The man entered a restricted area of the 30 Rockefeller Center studio while allegedly looking for co-host Al Roker, according to United Press International.

## Suspect allegedly shouted racial slur at NBC co-host; burglary charges expected

## Arrest made around 9 a.m.; suspect expected to face burglary and other charges, official says

The intruder, who has not been publicly named, entered the 30 Rockefeller Center plaza and slipped past the building's checkpoint system, according to United Press International. He allegedly made his way to a restricted studio area while looking for Al Roker, then lunged at Melvin, 47, and shouted a racial epithet.

The NYPD said officers took the man into custody at about 9 a.m. A law enforcement official told CNN that the suspect is expected to be charged with burglary and additional offenses.

Police are reviewing surveillance recordings and body camera video from the arresting officers to determine how the man evaded security, the official said.

Several NBC employees, who must swipe or show their work badges multiple times to enter the building, told CNN they were stunned by the breach. The network had bolstered security in February after the mother of anchor Savannah Guthrie was kidnapped. Today show hosts did not address the incident on the air.
