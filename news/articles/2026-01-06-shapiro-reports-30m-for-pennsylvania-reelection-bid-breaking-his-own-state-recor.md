The fundraising figure cements Shapiro's standing as one of the Democratic Party's most prominent governors as Pennsylvania draws national attention as the country's top presidential battleground, with Shapiro discussed as a potential 2028 White House contender.

Pennsylvania Democratic Gov. Josh Shapiro began 2026 with $30 million in his reelection campaign account, a new state record for cash on hand heading into an election year, his campaign said Tuesday.

The $30 million surpasses the previous Pennsylvania record of $13.4 million, which Shapiro reported at the start of 2022. That year, Shapiro went on to spend more than $70 million on the governor's race, setting what was then the state record for campaign expenditures in a single race.

Shapiro, 52, is not expected to face a serious challenge in the May 19 Democratic primary, according to AP. The deadline for candidates to file paperwork to appear on the primary ballot is March 10.

In the general election, Shapiro is expected to face Republican Stacy Garrity, Pennsylvania's twice-elected state treasurer, who has received the endorsement of the state Republican Party. Garrity had not yet filed campaign fundraising disclosures as of Tuesday; Pennsylvania candidates must submit those reports to the state by Jan. 31.

Pennsylvania has emerged as the nation's premier presidential battleground state, according to AP. Shapiro's 2022 victory elevated his profile within the Democratic Party, where he is viewed as a potential 2028 presidential contender, according to AP.
