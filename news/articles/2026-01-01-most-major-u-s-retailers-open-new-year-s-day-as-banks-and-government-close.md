Most major U.S. retailers will be open on New Year's Day, Thursday, Jan. 1, 2026, while government offices, courts, post offices, banks, and stock markets will remain closed for the federal holiday, the Associated Press reported. Hours vary by location, and shoppers should verify schedules with their local stores before heading out.

The holiday pattern splits retail and grocery access — most large chains stay open, often with modified hours — from government services, financial institutions, and standard package delivery, which are suspended for the day.

## Government and financial services

Government buildings, post offices, courts, and schools are closed on New Year's Day, according to the AP. U.S. stock markets and banks are also closed Thursday and will reopen Friday, Jan. 2.

## Package delivery

Standard FedEx and UPS pickup and delivery services will not be available on New Year's Day, the AP reported. Some critical services will be offered at certain locations; customers should check with their local FedEx or UPS store for details.

## Major retailers

Walmart, Target, Macy's, and Kohl's are all open on New Year's Day but may have modified hours, according to the AP. Shoppers should check each retailer's website or call ahead to confirm local store schedules.

CVS and Walgreens are also open on the holiday with possible modified hours. The AP noted that Walgreens customers should also check whether their local pharmacy is operating, as pharmacy hours may differ from general store hours.

## Grocery stores

Most national grocery chains — including Albertsons, Whole Foods, and Kroger — are open on New Year's Day but may have modified hours, the AP reported. In-store pharmacies at grocery locations tend to be closed for the holiday; shoppers should check with their specific store.

Several chains will be closed for the holiday. Aldi, Trader Joe's, and Costco will not be open on New Year's Day, according to the AP.
