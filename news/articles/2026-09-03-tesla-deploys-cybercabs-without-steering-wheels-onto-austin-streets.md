## Tesla deploys Cybercabs without steering wheels onto Austin streets

**Subtype:** fact

- Tesla deployed dozens of self-driving Cybercabs without steering wheels or brake pedals onto Austin, Texas, streets on Thursday, according to The Associated Press.
- The gold-colored vehicles give passengers no way to take control of the car in an emergency, AP reported.
- Tesla shares rose more than 5% Thursday on hopes that the futuristic-looking taxis will catch on quickly, AP said.
- Tesla teased the launch on X with the line "No steering wheel, no pedals," and CEO Elon Musk followed with posts showing a giant Cybercab above the Austin skyline and writing "A Storm of Cybercabs."
- AP reported Tesla plans to launch the service in other cities but said the rollout timeline remains unclear.

## Shares climb 5% on hopes futuristic taxis will catch on

Tesla promoted the launch on X with the line "No steering wheel, no pedals." Musk followed with a post showing a giant Cybercab floating above the Austin skyline and later wrote, "A Storm of Cybercabs."

AP reported that Tesla plans to expand the service to other cities but said the broader rollout timeline remains unclear and that Musk needs to move quickly. An invitation-only launch event was expected to be held later Thursday.
