## AP previews Tuesday's Massachusetts state primary

Massachusetts Democrats head to the polls Tuesday for a state primary in which U.S. Sen. Ed Markey faces a competitive challenge from U.S. Rep. Seth Moulton in his bid for a third full term, according to an Associated Press preview by Robert Yoon.

The AP described the race as the latest head-to-head matchup between the party's moderate and progressive wings. The contest, the AP reported, ties together two recurring themes in Democratic primaries this year: progressives versus moderates, and longtime incumbents versus significantly younger challengers.

This time, however, top progressives are backing the incumbent against a more moderate challenger, the AP reported.

Markey turned 80 in July and is the second-oldest sitting U.S. senator seeking reelection in 2026, the AP reported. He took his Senate seat in 2013 after serving nearly 37 years in the House. He last faced a competitive primary in 2020, when he defeated then-U.S. Rep. Joseph Kennedy III, 55% to 45%.

Bay State voters will also choose nominees Tuesday for U.S. House, governor, and a variety of state and local offices.


In the run-up to the primary, [Markey told a debate audience he uses "whatever comes up" on his iPhone or iPad when asked whether he relies on AI platforms](/articles/2026-08-30-markey-says-he-uses-whatever-comes-up-on-ipad-at-moulton-debate/), an answer Moulton supporters cited as reinforcing the generational argument at the center of his challenge.
