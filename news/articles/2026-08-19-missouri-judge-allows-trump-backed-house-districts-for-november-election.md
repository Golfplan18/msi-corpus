## Court rejects referendum bid; secretary of state had earlier thrown out petition

A Cole County Circuit Court judge ruled Wednesday that Missouri can use new U.S. House districts backed by President Donald Trump in the November elections, rejecting a bid to put the Republican-friendly map to a statewide vote of the people.

The ruling by Judge Daniel Green is a victory for Republicans seeking to hold onto their slim majority in the U.S. House. The Associated Press, which reported the ruling, said the court's decision is likely to be appealed.

The case reached the court after Republican Secretary of State Denny Hoskins rejected a petition with thousands of signatures that sought to force a November referendum on the new congressional map. According to the AP, Hoskins asserted that Missouri's Constitution does not allow a referendum on congressional redistricting. Green agreed with that position.

Missouri's new districts already were used in the state's August primaries, on the same day that Hoskins turned back the referendum petition. The August primary use of the map preceded Wednesday's ruling, which now confirms the map's place on the November ballot.
