## Dutch central bank relocates 86 metric tons of gold from North America to London

**Subtype:** fact

- De Nederlandsche Bank transferred approximately 86 metric tons of gold from vaults in New York and Ottawa to London between March and August 2026, the bank announced.
- The bank said the relocation was part of "crisis preparedness" in a time of global political unrest and aimed to improve the tradability of its reserves.
- Before the transfer, New York held 31.3% of the Dutch gold and Ottawa held 19.7%; both cities now hold 18.5% of the reserves.
- The Dutch central bank owns 612.4 metric tons of gold valued at 72.2 billion euros at the end of 2025.

## Gold move shifts reserve distribution away from New York and Ottawa

The bank owns a total of 612.4 metric tons, equivalent to roughly 675 tons, of gold. At the end of 2025, the reserve was valued at 72.2 billion euros, according to the announcement.

Before the transfer, the combined holdings in New York and Ottawa accounted for roughly 313 metric tons — just over half of the total reserve. The redistribution does not change the overall size of the reserve but shifts where a larger share is physically stored.

De Nederlandsche Bank did not identify specific events prompting the move, instead pointing to a general climate of uncertainty. "We expect that we will never need to use them, but we do need to strengthen our resilience and preparedness," Sleijpen said in a written statement.
