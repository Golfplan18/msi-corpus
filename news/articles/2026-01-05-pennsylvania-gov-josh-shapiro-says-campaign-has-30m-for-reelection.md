Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro’s campaign said Tuesday that it has $30 million on hand for his reelection bid, setting what it described as a new state record for a candidate heading into an election year.

The announcement comes as Shapiro seeks a second term in the general election this fall. The campaign said the amount eclipses the previous state record that Shapiro reported at the start of 2022.

The race is expected to put Shapiro, 52, against Stacy Garrity, the twice-elected state treasurer who has been endorsed by the state Republican Party, according to the report. Garrity had yet to report campaign fundraising details as of Tuesday.

Pennsylvania has emerged as a leading presidential battleground state, and Shapiro’s showing in the 2022 governor’s race has raised his profile within the Democratic Party, the report said, including among those who view him as a potential 2028 White House contender.

The report said the deadline to file campaign finance reports with the state is Jan. 31. For the May primary, Shapiro is not expected to face a serious challenge, and the deadline to submit paperwork to get on the primary election ballot is March 10.

The report said Shapiro’s $30 million figure surpasses his own earlier benchmark: he reported $13.4 million at the start of 2022, and later that year the report said he set a state campaign spending record by spending more than $70 million on the governor’s race.
