## Summary

**Subtype:** fact

- Two U.S. Navy jets collided midair on Sunday during the "Gunfighter Skies" air show at Mountain Home Air Force Base, approximately 57 miles southwest of Boise, Idaho.
- All four crew members ejected from their aircraft and deployed parachutes, landing safely.
- The two planes became sandwiched together in midair before the ejections, according to the Associated Press.
- The aircraft careened into a field after the collision and exploded into a fireball; all four crew members survived.

Two Navy jets collided midair Sunday afternoon during the "Gunfighter Skies" air show at Mountain Home Air Force Base, the Associated Press reported. The aircraft became entangled, with the two planes sandwiching together before the four crew members ejected and deployed their parachutes. The fliers descended safely as the wreckage plunged into a field and exploded. The base is located about 57 miles (92 kilometers) southwest of Boise.

MSI <a href='/articles/2026-05-19-2-navy-jets-collide-at-idaho-air-show-all-four-crew-eject-safely'>previously reported</a> that all four crew members ejected safely after the two jets collided. The Associated Press noted that the planes were sandwiched together in the air before the ejections.
