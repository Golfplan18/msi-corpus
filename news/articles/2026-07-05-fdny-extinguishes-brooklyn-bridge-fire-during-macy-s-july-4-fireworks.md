## FDNY extinguishes Brooklyn Bridge fire during Macy's July 4 fireworks

**Subtype:** fact

- The FDNY extinguished a fire that broke out on the Brooklyn Bridge during the Macy's July 4th Fireworks Show on Saturday.
- No injuries were reported. The blaze was put out by two fire engines before 10 p.m., according to the FDNY.
- The bridge was closed to traffic during the fireworks display, the FDNY said. A department spokesperson said such fires are not unexpected and are why officials keep crowds at a distance during fireworks.
- Severe weather threats prompted the show to start early and led to the evacuation of the National Mall in Washington, D.C.

## Severe weather threat prompts early start, Mall evacuation

The fire began shortly after the Macy's 4th of July Fireworks Show started just after 9 p.m., video footage showed. Flames and smoke rose from the bridge, which had been closed to traffic during the display. The FDNY said two fire engines were used to extinguish the blaze, and firefighters were seen dousing the flames shortly before 10 p.m.

A department spokesperson said such fires are not unexpected and are why officials keep crowds at a distance during fireworks, according to the Associated Press.

The show was moved up early because of the threat of severe weather that canceled and delayed other celebrations along the East Coast, the AP reported. In Washington, D.C., a storm prompted an evacuation of crowdgoers on the National Mall.

No injuries were reported.
