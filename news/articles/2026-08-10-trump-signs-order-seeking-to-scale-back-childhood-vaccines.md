## Trump signs order seeking to scale back childhood vaccines

- Trump signed an executive order urging parents to spread out childhood vaccines in more visits to pediatricians' offices.
- The order encourages states to mandate fewer vaccines for school attendance, the Wall Street Journal reported.
- The administration will seek alternatives to aluminum, a common vaccine ingredient, under the order.
- Trump at an Oval Office event repeated previous comments linking childhood shots and autism — a connection that scientists have said is unfounded, according to the Wall Street Journal.
- The Wall Street Journal reported it was unclear how the vaccine recommendations would be implemented.

## Trump repeats autism-vaccine link scientists call unfounded at signing event

Trump signed the executive order at an Oval Office event where he discussed previous comments linking childhood shots and autism. Scientists have said the connection is unfounded, according to the Wall Street Journal.

The order urges parents to space vaccines across more pediatrician visits and encourages states to mandate fewer vaccines for school attendance. A third provision directs the administration to find alternatives to aluminum, which the Journal described as a common vaccine ingredient.

The Wall Street Journal reported that it is unusual, if not unprecedented, for detailed vaccine policy to emerge from the White House. It was not clear how the vaccine recommendations would be implemented.
