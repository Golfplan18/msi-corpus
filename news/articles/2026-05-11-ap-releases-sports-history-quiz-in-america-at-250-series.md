## Summary

**Subtype:** fact

- The Associated Press released a sports history quiz on May 12, 2026, as part of its “America at 250” series.
- The quiz tests knowledge of U.S. sports history, covering a range of American sports milestones.
- Holly Meyer, a member of the AP’s Global Religion team, wrote the quiz for the America at 250 initiative.

The quiz, published on the AP website, invites readers to test their knowledge of iconic moments in American sports. It is one of several interactive features the wire service has produced for the America at 250 project, which examined various facets of the nation’s history and culture in the lead-up to July 4, 2026.

Meyer, who typically covers religion, contributed the sports history quiz as part of a cross-departmental effort within the AP to produce topical quizzes for the anniversary series. The piece is available on the AP News site under the America at 250 section.
