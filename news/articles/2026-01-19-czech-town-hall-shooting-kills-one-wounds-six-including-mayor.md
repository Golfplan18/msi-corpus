Czech authorities ruled out terrorism, extremism, and political or religious motivation, saying the attack appeared to stem from a personal dispute. The shooting drew condolences from both the country's president and prime minister.

A gunman opened fire inside a town hall in northern Czech Republic on Monday, killing one employee and wounding six others — including the town's mayor and three police officers — before fatally shooting himself, police said.

The shooting took place at the Chribska town hall. Police said there was no further public danger after the shooter's death.

## Motive and weapons

Czech police ruled out terrorism and an extremist attack. "Nor is it a politically or religiously motivated attack. Everything indicates that it is a relationship-based motive," a police statement said.

Police said the attacker was armed with three illegally held weapons.

## Official response

Czech President Petr Pavel and Prime Minister Andrej Babiš offered condolences to the victims' relatives following the attack.

## About Chribska

Chribska is a village of about 1,300 residents situated near the German border in the Ústí nad Labem region of northern Czech Republic.
